BOUNDARY
   These are separate scientific arguments, populations, result sets, and builds.
   Do not combine their metrics, evidence references, conclusions, or PDFs into
   an averaged paper. An explicitly cited analogy or cross-reference may help
   either domain, but it supplies no empirical validation for the other domain.
   Human surveys/profiles are not SAFE AI experiments. SQLite effect tests are
   not human evidence, sandbox verification, or external exactly-once results.

ARCHIVAL PRESERVATION
   Supplied TeX/ZIP/PDF files under tips/ and project/brain/tips/ are archival
   originals; their bytes are immutable and verified by tips/manifest.json.
   Maintain revised text in docs/sections/.

AGENT OWNERSHIP
   Work in a separate permitted worktree. State which paper and files you own in
   the coordination log. Never interpret a change to one paper as authorization
   to rewrite the other. Read the other paper only where a specific connection
   could strengthen the argument; label that connection explicitly.

Environmental context in The Minds We Choose

Scope and source preservation
Run from the minds-integration checkout root. Human sources are in
scripts/brain and docs/sections/brain. Supplied sources under project/brain/tips
and tips are read-only. SAFE AI results and manuscripts are separate.
All transient input, output, logs and builds stay under this checkout's target/.

Interpretation
Alignment is chronological context, not a causal estimate. Do not fit event-date
breakpoints or assign significance to overlap. Keep the disputed genetic inference
separate from measured climate evidence and regional demographic interpretations.
The scientific account does not depend on accepting the bottleneck or assigning
a hominin consequence to the impact. The SI records a prior-tektite-count
inconsistency, and not every quoted impact-age comparison separately restates its
uncertainty convention. These limitations remain in the register and supplement.

\textbf{Abstract.}\enspace
Making an AI system smarter at searching candidate actions ($K_{\mathrm{opt}}$) neither grants
it authority to execute them nor keeps internal safety judges reliable: any shared classifier
blindspot $J>0$ fails with probability $1-(1-J)^{K_{\mathrm{opt}}}\to 1$ as search widens, while
preference-uncertainty shutdown compliance vanishes as belief uncertainty $\sigma_H\to 0$. We
separate internal capability and reward aim from \emph{conferred, revocable authority} enforced
at exogenous capability-bearing sinks via canonical wire bijections, Ed25519 quorum fences,
$d$-hop attenuated credentials, relational mutexes, and monotone safe-bootstrap ratchets
(\VTwoProofs{} verified Verus obligations across \VTwoExecFns{} executable functions and
\VTwoTheorems{} theorems; \VFiveProofMutationsRejected/\VFiveProofMutationsRejected{} soundness
mutants rejected). Across \VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes{} scaling episodes ($K_{\mathrm{opt}}\le
\VFiveOptMaxWidth$) and a three-process SQLite write-ahead-logged cluster
(\VFiveClusterMedianUs\,$\mu$s median commit), exogenous sinks hold breaches at $0.0\%$ while
harnessing untrusted $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$ search in a zero-authority sandbox closes vulnerability
routes $\VFiveRatchetMaxSpeedup\times$ faster than human-only review ($0$ breaches). Moreover,
training policy gradients \emph{inside} pre-commit gates above threshold $q_{\mathrm{gate}}^*$
collapses covert unmonitored shortcutting ($+\VFourNeuralGateZeroGapPp\to
+\VFourNeuralGateSeventyFiveGapPp$\,pp) while lifting task competence $+\VFourNeuralTrainInsideGainPp$\,pp
over post-hoc filtering, and two-stage escrow triage cuts 5-step AgentDojo benign false aborts
$\VFourTrajEscrowReductionFactor\times$ ($\VFourTrajIsoFalseBlockPct\%\to
\VFourTrajEscrowFalseBlockPct\%$; $\VFourTrajEscrowFastPathPct\%$ unpaused fast path). Across
\VFiveStatGrandPairs{} paired live Qwen3-4B/8B and AgentDojo runs, stateful mediation achieves
$0/\VFiveStatGrandPairs$ breaches versus $\VFiveStatGrandBaseLeaks/\VFiveStatGrandPairs$
unmediated (Suissa--Shuster $p=\VFiveStatGrandSuissaSci$, Ville $\log_{10}E=\VFiveStatVilleLogTenE$),
while pilot $N{=}\VFourHoldoutCasesPerCheckpoint$ Bonferroni bounds, database/OS refinement,
uncooperative APIs, and frontier/human trials remain open.

\textbf{Abstract.}\enspace
AI control protocols act on proposals: a monitor reads what an agent proposes, and an agent that is caught is stopped.
Authority, however, is exercised in state, and stopping the model does not stop the effects it has already dispatched.
We hold authority as state checked where effects commit, and compare three stops: halting inference, revoking admission
at a broker, and fencing the sink so that it refuses, at commit, work issued before the stop. On real broker and sink
processes driven by a synthetic agent, \vsixHaltRange{} effects committed after a halt took effect, \vsixRevokeRange{}
after a revocation and \vsixFenceRange{} after a fence (by design), which discarded \vsixFenceLost{} legitimate calls in
flight. Counted from the decision, a halt leaks about \(\lambda(L+w)\) (call rate \(\lambda\), enforcement delay \(L\),
tool latency \(w\)); a fence removes \(\lambda w\), and only faster enforcement removes what commits during \(L\). Both
hold only for effects that take place at commit. A reviewer that does not reason misses violations that live in state:
given the rules and the state, a \vsixEFiveCkpt{} reviewer replying with a bare verdict accepted, in all
\vsixExactCases{} cases, an approved proposal that the state had since made unauthorized; reasoning (median
\vsixReasonerTokens{} tokens) brought this to \vsixReasonerExact. A gate that reads state instead refuses work its
specification did not foresee. On AgentDojo, a specification written without the benchmark's solutions admitted the
reference solution of \vsixStageStrictUser{} tasks; under it a \vsixEEightCkpt{} agent completed \vsixStrictUtility{}
benign tasks, against \vsixUnprotectedUtility{} unprotected and \vsixProgentUtility{} under Progent, and injections
written to stay inside it succeeded in \vsixStrictInside. Of \vsixFenceMechanisms{} fencing mechanisms on PostgreSQL,
etcd and a filesystem, only \vsixFenceAllThree{} was enforced by the surface, refused a forged epoch and held while
stale writers ran. All models are small open-weight checkpoints run locally.

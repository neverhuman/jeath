\subsection{Invariant Omissions and Snapshot Non-Composition}
Exhaustive finite-state exploration of our 22-action reference model
(Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}, Table~\ref{tab:results}) visits \CheckedStates{} reachable
states and \CheckedEdges{} transition checks with zero declared violations and $20/20$
positive tasks passing, whereas nine single-clause mutations each produce a shortest
counterexample. Removing only the aggregate-budget clause while keeping per-action limits
expands the accepted graph to \OmittedStates{} states (\OmittedEdges{} checks) with zero
declared violations yet spends $8$ units against an intended allowance of $2$---proving
that neither positive tests nor exhaustive checking detects an omitted protected
interest \cite{moon}.

Concurrent snapshot checking fails even when aggregate limits remain in the specification.
Over all \VThreeCompHundredTriples{} integer triples $(c, q_1, q_2) \in \{0,\dots,100\}^3$
at budget $B = 100$, \VThreeCompHundredAdmissible{} pairs satisfy per-request snapshot
admissibility ($c + q_1 \le B$ and $c + q_2 \le B$), yet \VThreeCompHundredOverspend{}
(\VThreeCompHundredOverspendPct\%, matching $\frac{B}{2B+3}$ in
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}) jointly overspend, versus \VThreeCompHundredSerializedOverspend{}
under serialized reservation. Across our separate-store omission model
(\path{ablate_v2.py}), omitting any of ten mechanisms individually or in all $45$ pairs
yields concrete violation witnesses in all \VTwoAblationWitnesses/\VTwoAblationSets{}
omission sets across \VTwoTraces{} searched traces.

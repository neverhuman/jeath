\begin{abstract}
Human partner and ally choice must evaluate \emph{performance before outcomes}---committing years before juvenile survival or lifelong fidelity can be observed. Across twenty-four propositions and fifteen datasets spanning \FosN{} hominin crania, cross-cultural ethnography, laboratory encounters, longitudinal couples, multimodal facial attribution, and \PboThreePlatformRecords{} platform records, we resolve when the human brain grew versus stalled, why \emph{primary} pair-bond devotion outranks \emph{secondary} mating criteria, and what separates earned outlier confidence from gross arrogance. First, synchronizing fossil crania with the 57-core \texttt{LR04} $\delta^{18}\mathrm{O}$ stack shows brain volume climbed to $\sim\!\PboPlateauMeanCC\ccm$ by $1.50\,\mathrm{Ma}$, stalled for $800\,\mathrm{kyr}$ ($\PboGapCoolingKyr$--$\PboGapTektiteKyr\,\mathrm{kyr}$ before $1.12\,\mathrm{Ma}$ cooling, the contested $930$--$813\,\mathrm{ka}$ bottleneck, or $789\,\mathrm{ka}$ impact ejecta), and surged $+50\%/\mathrm{Myr}$ across Africa and Eurasia only after $1.0$--$0.78\,\mathrm{Ma}$ cooking and biparental/alloparental provisioning overcame the age-4.5 brain peak (\PboChildPeakRmr{} resting energy; \PboSccsChildSoloMomPct{} solo-mother care across 136 societies). Second, single-context stranger markets select on \emph{secondary criteria} with high instant legibility---portrait rank (\PboIrreducibleGapFiveHundred{} consensus gap beyond \texttt{HoloFace-L1}), $6'0''$ cutoffs (null fertility $r=+\PboLidborgHeightRepR$), income brackets, hobby tags ($r=\PboHobbyObjR$), and demographic sorting---while compressing pre-romance witness audits $\PboHcmstAuditRatio\times$ (tripling breakup when cohabitation rushes $\le 3$ months). Inside unions across 175 countries and \PboJoelDatasetsN{} longitudinal studies, secondary criteria collapse near zero while \emph{primary criteria}---kindness ($\PboChkKindSD\,\SD$, beating checklist fit $\PboChkKindFitRatio\times$ and politics/class/degree homophily $\PboHomoPolRatio\text{--}\PboHomoEduRatio\times$), live play, nine-domain mind breadth ($\PboOkcDomGain\times$ validity), mutual reverence, and hearth devotion---govern love, marital stability, and the $\VgMarrShare\%$ non-marriage share of U.S.\ fertility decline. Finally, because coalition standing multiplies verified agency by prosocial delivery ($\widehat W=\hat b\hat p$), warm outlier confidence wins across live dates (\PboWarmOutlierYesW{}/\PboWarmOutlierYesM{}), 175 countries ($\PboKowWarmOverHumblePP\,\mathrm{pp}$ male partnering; $\PboKowWarmKindGainM$/$\PboKowWarmKindGainW$ partner kindness), and \PboSccsEgalPersuadePct{}-persuasive egalitarian bands, whereas mature juries crush unbacked boasting $\PboOverclaimAgeSurge\times$ harder and role-congruity plus status-threat priors impose an executive double bind on women ($\PboColdBacklashRatio\times$ coldness drop; $\PboOutsmartWomanOnManPP\,\mathrm{pp}$ outsmarting threat; \PboExecDivW{} vs.\ \PboExecDivM{} CEO divorce) resolved by co-protagonist framing and caliber assortment.
\end{abstract}

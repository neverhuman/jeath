\begin{abstract}
Hominin brain volume roughly tripled over three million years despite steep metabolic cost and slow per-generation change ($\sim\!10^{-4}\SD$). Synchronizing three open cranial compilations with the 57-core \texttt{LR04} benthic $\delta^{18}\mathrm{O}$ paleoclimate record shows that the 1.5--0.7\,Ma endocranial stasis plateau ($\sim\!\PboPlateauMeanCC\ccm$) began $\sim\!\PboGapCoolingKyr$--$\PboGapTektiteKyr\,\mathrm{kyr}$ before 1.12\,Ma European glacial depopulation, the contested 930--813\,ka coalescent bottleneck, and the 789\,ka Australasian impact, consistent with an energetic ceiling relaxed near 1.0--0.78\,Ma controlled fire and cooking. Because provisioning and cooperation unfold years after union formation, we derive twenty propositions decomposing what pre-outcome observation \emph{reveals}, what partners \emph{deliver}, and how life stages \emph{allocate} reproduction. Across \SdRows{} speed dates, strangers weight legible looks over unreadable intelligence and sincerity, stalling at a single-context ceiling ($R^2_{\infty,1}=\PboTwoWaySingleAsym$) that multi-context camp juries break ($R^2_{8,8}=\PboTwoWayEightEight$). Male stature percentiles plateau near the 72nd percentile ($\beta_2=\PboWhyteQuadBeta$), heap $\PboHeightSixZeroRatio\times$ at $6'0''$ before plunging $-\PboHeightCliffDrop$ at $6'1''$, and show null meta-analytic fertility ($r=+\PboLidborgHeightRepR$). Across \PboCombinedRecords{} platform records, our 43-wave \texttt{HoloFace-L1} ensemble reaches Test MAE $\PboHoloFullTestMAE$ ($\rho=\PboHoloFullSpearman$; zero-metadata \texttt{HoloFace-Pure} $\rho=\PboHoloPureSpearman$, $\PboShapAnatomyTotal$ anatomical/photometric Shapley share) with regionally invariant 106-landmark facial beauty drivers ($r=\PboFaceWorldCorrW/\PboFaceWorldCorrM$), yet leaves $\PboIrreducibleGapFiveHundred$--$\PboIrreducibleGapAll$ of infinite-voter consensus unexplained. Meanwhile, childless ``Want children'' flips by midlife across five countries while female commitment stays higher, and $\VgMarrShare\%$ of recent U.S.\ young-adult birth decline sits in non-marriage.
\end{abstract}

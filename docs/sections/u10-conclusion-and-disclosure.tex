\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Capability measures what an optimizer can find; authority governs what effects an institution lets commit and what exposure survives when permission is withdrawn. Across five evidence tiers, we have separated claims that hold over all proposal policies within a verified core ($\VTwoProofs$ Verus obligations across $\VTwoExecFns$ executable predicates and $\VTwoTheorems$ trace theorems), claims validated on separate-process local SQLite and Linux confinement boundaries ($\VTwoFaults$ fault histories, $\VTwoLinearizedRuns/\VTwoLinearizedRuns$ bounded concurrent linearizations, $\VTwoProbes/\VTwoProbes$ kernel probes), exact combinatorial and statistical bounds on pathway width, report-only non-identifiability, monitor correlation, and selection geometry, and obligations that remain open until executed on held-out checkpoints and accountable institutions. \emph{Intelligence proposes; institutions confer, bound, and revoke reach. Winning the safety race requires clearing a verifiable bar and sealing external cutsets, not gambling for rank or trusting unrevocable disposition.}

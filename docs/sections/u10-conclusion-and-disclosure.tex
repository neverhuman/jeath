\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Capability measures what an optimizer can find; authority governs what effects an institution lets commit and what exposure survives when permission is withdrawn. Across five evidence tiers, we have separated claims proved over all proposal policies within a verified Rust kernel ($\VTwoProofs$ Verus obligations across $\VTwoExecFns$ executable functions---including concrete \texttt{ExecBrokerKernel}/\texttt{ExecSinkKernel} forward-simulation refinement and wire injectivity---and $\VTwoTheorems$ proof theorems), claims validated on separate-process SQLite, \texttt{AF\_UNIX} partition schedules, Linux confinement, and CPU \texttt{Qwen3-4B}/\texttt{8B} checkpoints ($\VTwoFaults$ fault histories, $\VTwoLinearizedRuns/\VTwoRaceRuns$ $1..\VTwoLinearizedMaxActors$-actor linearizations, $0/\text{\VFourJepsenSchedulesPerClass}$ cooperative Jepsen post-ack leaks, $\VTwoProbes/\VTwoProbes$ kernel probes, and $\VFourHoldoutTotalCampaigns$ frozen holdout campaigns scaling safe-useful completion from $\VFourFourBCompleteSafeUsefulPct\%$ to $\VFourEightBCompleteSafeUsefulPct\%$ with $0.0\%$ unauthorized effects), structural measurements on operational AgentDojo/Linux cutsets, $\VFourTrajEvalCount$ 5-step trajectory monitors, and 412-parameter neural RL post-training, and full-scale $N \ge \VTwoZeroLossCasesMin$ checkpoint, frontier, and human-governance obligations that remain open.

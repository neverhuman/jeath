\section{Conclusion: Grounded Hope Without Reading Machine Minds}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Capability measures what an optimizer can discover; authority governs what external effects
an institution allows to commit and what exposure survives when permission is withdrawn. As
candidate search width $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$, subagent coalitions $K_{\mathrm{sub}}$, and
inference speed scale into superintelligent regimes, any defense whose miss rate $J > 0$
relies on behavioral virtue, shared-transcript judges, or prompt-level compliance collapses
toward certainty of breach via $1 - (1 - J)^{K_{\mathrm{opt}}}$. Separating internal
intelligence from conferred, revocable authority offers grounded hope—not by assuming
superintelligent systems will share human motives, but by proving that institutions do not
have to read machine minds in order to bound and constructively harness machine reach:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Optimization-invariant boundaries and safe-bootstrap acceleration (Tiers~1--2).}
Across $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \le \VFiveOptMaxWidth$ ($0/\VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes$ exogenous
breaches), canonical wire bijections, conservative reservation envelopes, $d$-hop
attenuation, relational mutexes, and quorum lease fences hold external invariants flat,
contract session covert signaling $\VFiveCovertSessionContraction\times$
($\VFiveCovertSessionTypedBits$ vs.\ $\VFiveCovertSessionProseBits$\,bits at
$T{=}\VFiveCovertSessionMaxCalls$), and enforce $100\%$ post-fence shutdown across all
belief precisions $\sigma_H \ge 0$ ($\gamma^*(4)=\VFiveOffSwitchGammaStar$), backed by
$\VTwoProofs$ verified Verus obligations across $\VTwoExecFns$ executable functions and
$\VTwoTheorems$ theorems ($\VFiveProofMutationsRejected/\VFiveProofMutationsRejected$
mutants rejected). Crucially, confining untrusted $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$ search to a
zero-authority sandbox with polynomial cut verification flips capability scaling into a
$\VFiveRatchetMaxSpeedup\times$ faster defense ratchet at $0$ live breaches.
\item \textbf{Constructive RL realignment, live capability gains, and low-friction escrow (Tiers~3--4).}
Training neural policy gradients \emph{inside} pre-commit gates above $q_{\mathrm{gate}}^*$
collapses evaluation-aware covert shortcutting ($+\VFourNeuralGateZeroGapPp\to
+\VFourNeuralGateSeventyFiveGapPp$\,pp) while lifting useful task completion
$+\VFourNeuralTrainInsideGainPp$\,pp ($+\VFourNeuralTrainInsideUnmonGainPp$\,pp unmonitored)
over post-hoc deployment filtering. Across $\VFiveStatGrandPairs$ paired live Qwen3-4B/8B
and AgentDojo runs, stateful mediation records $0/\VFiveStatGrandPairs$ unauthorized commits
versus $\VFiveStatGrandBaseLeaks/\VFiveStatGrandPairs$ unmediated (Suissa--Shuster
$p=\VFiveStatGrandSuissaSci$, Ville $\log_{10} E = \VFiveStatVilleLogTenE$) while
safe-and-useful completion doubles from Qwen3-4B to Qwen3-8B, three-process SQLite clusters
survive $\VFiveClusterFuzzFrames$ fuzz frames and $\VFiveClusterSigkillCycles$ hard
process-kill cycles at $\VFiveClusterMedianUs\,\mu$s median latency, and two-stage escrow
triage cuts 5-step benign false aborts $\VFourTrajEscrowReductionFactor\times$
($\VFourTrajIsoFalseBlockPct\%\to\VFourTrajEscrowFalseBlockPct\%$) with
$\VFourTrajEscrowFastPathPct\%$ unpaused fast-path execution.
\item \textbf{Explicit negative boundaries and open Tier-5 obligations.}
Every claim is paired with its exact failure boundary: verified kernels faithfully enforce
omitted specifications, uncooperative external APIs duplicate or leak effects, signature
patching leaves $94.7\%$--$99.9\%$ of AgentDojo routes live, Good--Turing coverage reads
$\VThreeCovWithheldEstPct\%$ under total critical withholding, and pilot
$N{=}\VFourHoldoutCasesPerCheckpoint < \VTwoZeroLossCasesMin$ usefulness noninferiority
($\le\VTwoNoninferiorityMarginPp$\,pp), database/OS formal refinement, frontier-scale RLHF,
and human conferral and territorial consent trials remain open.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Intelligence proposes; institutions confer, bound, and revoke reach. By placing
revocable capability sinks outside the optimizer, training policies inside those gates, and
admitting untrusted search only through verified defense ratchets, stronger intelligence can
deepen competence and close vulnerabilities without ever acquiring unrevocable authority.}

\section{Conclusion and Scope Disclosure}\label{sec:conclusion}

Intelligence measures how capably a system searches, predicts, and plans; authority determines which irreversible transitions an external sink will commit. Treating safety as an internal property of model weights or transcript classifiers fails whenever queued operations outlive broker revocation, candidate search scales across blindspots, exploit routes multiply through tool composition, or deferential demeanor induces wider human grants. Moving authority outside the proposer---through Verus-verified capability transitions, \(\delta_{\max}\)-buffered quorum sink fences, narrow kernel and schema waists, prose-stripped escrow triage, and exogenous scope caps---turns each of those failure modes into an explicit, auditable engineering boundary.

Equally important, exogenous mediation improves practical agent usefulness rather than trading capability for refusal. Across frozen Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B campaigns, complete sink mediation achieves the highest safe-and-useful task completion with zero benign false blocks, eliminates late commits after authority withdrawal, and preserves Upgrade-Invariant soundness (\S\ref{sec:gaps}) as proposer competence doubles from 4B to 8B. Where cooperative preconditions exist or can be shimmed (\(\VEightFenceCensusCooperativeSum/\VEightFenceCensusTotal\) audited production APIs), containment rests on sink invariants quantified over all inputs rather than trust in model intent.

\para{Scope disclosure.} Our SQLite broker and cooperative sinks mediate only their own declared database and socket state transitions. Their tests do not constitute operating-system or storage-engine formal refinement, external exactly-once execution on uncooperative third-party APIs, frontier-scale reinforcement learning, or human-participant behavioral evidence. Live language-model campaigns use quantized open-weight Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B checkpoints on CPU hardware at pilot sample sizes (\(N = \VFourHoldoutCasesPerCheckpoint\) frozen holdout cases and \(N = \VFiveStatDojoClustTasks\) AgentDojo base tasks), where predeclared marginal union-bound intervals remain inconclusive. Selection, neural policy-gradient, and grant-reversal experiments validate mathematical incentive mechanics on controlled environments rather than deployed human institutions.

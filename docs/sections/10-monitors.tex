\section{New synthetic studies of the premises}
\label{sec:monitors}

\subsection{Classifiers trained from scratch}
The earlier drafts report six-family results from absent companion scripts. We do not treat those values as reproduced evidence. The supplied Rust study instead trains small classifiers from scratch on an eight-dimensional nonlinear synthetic labeling task. It runs eight independent task/training seeds with 600 examples per classifier, 25 shuffled stochastic-gradient epochs, 800 separate benign calibration examples, and 1,000 held-out hazardous episodes per arm. Every gate's threshold targets a nominal 5\% false-alarm rate on its own calibration sample; this does not enforce exactly 5\% on the evaluation sample.

The shared-family arm uses six eight-hidden-unit tanh networks. The mixed arm alternates three of those networks with three logistic regressions trained on their own samples. There are two classifier families, not the six in the missing study. Individual strength is not matched across families, so the study does not identify a causal benefit of model diversity. A separately trained neural surrogate guides 12 rounds of eight random local candidate edits, constrained to Euclidean radius 1.5, the original input cube, and the true hazardous class. It does not query the defended gates in the transfer-only arms. The labels are harmless Boolean fixtures with no real hazard content.

We compare the same held-out input across gates with independently sampled hazardous inputs for each gate. An additional arm chooses the lowest worst-gate margin from eight surrogate-edited candidates using eight full-pipeline feedback queries per episode. Those queries change the attacker and evaluation distribution; they are not extra independent episodes. Training, calibration, and held-out samples are disjoint, but all use the same known synthetic task family. There is no sealed distribution-shift validation against a frontier agent.

Joint rates, marginal products, conditional misses after earlier misses, and benign false blocks are recorded per seed and at every depth. Uncertainty is summarized across the independent seeds; the 1.96-standard-error bands are descriptive approximations with only eight seeds, not exact intervals or uniform opponent bounds. The independently sampled arm factorizes in its population conditional on fixed task, models and surrogate. Finite episode counts can differ substantially from a small product prediction; agreement need not be exact. Pooling tasks before multiplying also changes the estimand. None of these arms establishes independent failures along a coordinated real campaign, or a positive asymptotic floor.

\subsection{Reporting, cuts, and reward shape}
The graph fixture computes paths by topological dynamic programming and internal vertex cuts by a separate split-vertex max-flow solver. Both supplied eight-stage examples have 65,536 paths, but minimum cuts four and two. Removing the computed cut eliminates all reported paths. Adding one unreported one-vertex bypass leaves one path open, explicitly distinguishing graph correctness from graph completeness. Brute-force enumeration of small DAGs checks the cut solver against an independent method.

The planted-audit fixture separately estimates rejection power for a known 0.8 baseline recall, 40 or 200 plants, and several plant-recognition probabilities over 2,000 repetitions. A fixed one-sided binomial test at 0.05 is used. Recognizable plants weaken detection by construction; neither high power on these plants nor a coverage estimate bounds unknown omitted families.

Two reward-selection illustrations reconstruct the earlier drafts' stated mechanisms with new seeds and outputs. The first evolves capacity and taking traits in groups of five under holdings or contribution scores, two selection intensities, and explicit gate strengths. The second compares top-decile private-return selection, top-decile selection with a veto after harm, and reproduction proportional to capped delivered welfare. Its gamble has lower expected private return and a stipulated additional loss of 20 with probability 0.05 times gamble share. These premises encode the concern being illustrated; they are not evidence that a corresponding reward will train a caring assistant.

\section{Exogenous Boundaries Remain Invariant Under Superintelligent Optimization}
\label{sec:theory}

We now formalize why pre-deployment behavioral testing cannot certify disposition, why
endogenous classifiers and utility-uncertainty shutdown schemes degrade with capability,
and how exogenous sink mediation remains invariant across search width, subagent
collusion, covert signaling, and preference certainty. Proofs appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.

\subsection{Audit Volume and Uncertainty Contraction}

Consider an unrestricted policy facing $M \ge 2$ deployment decisions after $N$
pre-deployment test draws, defecting selectively on a recognizable context set $E$ having
deployment probability $\beta \in (0,1)$ and test probability $\beta/\Lambda$
($\Lambda \ge 1$).

\begin{proposition}[Disposition audit volume; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:audit}
At $\beta = (\ln 2)/M$, a policy defecting exclusively on $E$ defects at least once
across $M$ conditionally independent deployment decisions with probability
$1-(1-\beta)^M \ge 1/2$. Ensuring detection probability at least $1-\alpha$
($\alpha \in (0,1)$) across $N$ conditional Bernoulli test draws requires
\begin{equation}
N \;\ge\; \frac{\Lambda M}{2\ln 2}\ln(1/\alpha).
\label{eq:u03:audit}
\end{equation}
If $\Prb_{\mathrm{test}}(E)=0$, no finite $N$ detects $E$; if test campaigns are
positively correlated, effective volume shrinks to $N_{\mathrm{eff}} < N$.
\end{proposition}

Because $N$ scales linearly with $\Lambda M$, pre-release behavioral testing cannot
certify long-horizon alignment. Only runtime mediation before external commitment enforces
$\Lambda = 1$ across every consequential transition. Let $x_t \in X$ denote true world
and ledger state, $a_t \in \A$ an arbitrary proposal, $u_t \in U$ the executed primitive
operation, and $w_t \in W$ exogenous disturbances under $x_{t+1} \in F(x_t, u_t, w_t)$.
Maintaining a sound state-uncertainty envelope $\B_t \subseteq X$ ($x_t \in \B_t$)
relative to safety invariant $\I \subseteq X$ defines the robustly admissible operations
\cite{schneider,shield}:
\begin{equation}
U_{\I}(\B) \;=\; \Bigl\{u \in U : \bigcup_{x\in\B,\,w\in W} F(x,u,w) \subseteq \I \Bigr\}.
\label{eq:u03:admissible}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Robust mediation \& uncertainty contraction; Tier 2]
\label{u03:cor:contraction}
If $x_0 \in \I$, $x_t \in \B_t$ at every decision, every committed operation belongs to
$U_{\I}(\B_t)$ (else a verified $\I$-preserving fallback executes), and complete
mediation holds, then $x_t \in \I$ for all $t$ under every proposal policy, and
\begin{equation}
\B \subseteq \B' \;\Longrightarrow\; U_{\I}(\B') \subseteq U_{\I}(\B).
\label{eq:u03:contraction}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

Equation~\eqref{eq:u03:contraction} enforces a strict law: \emph{greater state
uncertainty can only contract, never enlarge, admissible authority.} Exhaustive
verification over budget $B=\VThreeUncBudget$ checks all \VThreeUncNestedPairs{} nested
interval pairs $[l_1,u_1]\subseteq [l_2,u_2]$ with \VThreeUncCounterexamples{}
counterexamples, and across lags $\Delta t \in \{0,1,2,4,8\}$ (\VThreeUncEpisodes{}
episodes/cell; Figure~\ref{fig:v3saturation}a), gating on sound $\B_t$ yields
\VThreeUncConsViolations{} breaches while contracting admission from
$\VThreeUncConsAdmLagZeroPct\%$ to $\VThreeUncConsAdmLagEightPct\%$ (versus
$\VThreeUncStaleViolLagFourPct\%$ stale-snapshot breaches at $\Delta t=4$ and
$\VThreeUncSelfRepViolLagZeroPct\%$ proposer self-report breaches at $\Delta t=0$).

\subsection{Superintelligence Scaling: Search Width, Smurfing, Covert Channels, and Corrigibility}

We next compare endogenous alignment defenses against exogenous sink mediation across
four superintelligence-regime axes (Tiers~2 and~4; Figures~\ref{fig:v5hero}b
and~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}, \path{superintelligence_scaling.json}).

\begin{theorem}[Optimization-pressure invariance vs.\ endogenous guard collapse; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:optinv}
Let a proposer evaluate $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \ge 1$ candidate actions per decision.
(i)~Against any endogenous transcript or learned guard stack with non-zero shared
blindspot probability $J \in (0,1)$ over invariant-violating payloads, best-of-$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$
selection achieves breach probability $1 - (1-J)^{K_{\mathrm{opt}}} \to 1$ as
$K_{\mathrm{opt}} \to \infty$. (ii)~Against a mediated cooperative sink enforcing
$U_{\I}(\B_t)$ via a canonical wire bijection and durable epoch fence, interface breach
probability across all $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$-step proposal policies is identically $0$,
leaving total risk bounded by the $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$-independent physical/TCB bypass
floor $u_{\mathrm{TCB}}$. (iii)~If $K$ colluding subagents split an unauthorized payload
$\Delta$ into $\Delta/K$ fragments under per-instance anomaly monitors of noise $\sigma$,
per-agent signal-to-noise collapses as $\mathrm{SNR}(K) = \Delta/(\sigma K)$, whereas a
shared reservation ledger with $d$-hop monotone attenuation preserves aggregate spend
$\le B$ and severs entire subtrees upon ancestor revocation.
\end{theorem}

Sweeping $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \in \{1,\dots,\VFiveOptMaxWidth\}$ across
\VFiveOptEpisodesPerWidth{} episodes per width (\VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes{} total
exogenous episodes; Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}b), a three-layer transcript stack
($J=0.65\%$) degrades from $\VFiveOptTranscriptBasePct\%$ breach at $K_{\mathrm{opt}}=1$
to $\VFiveOptTranscriptMaxPct\%$ at $K_{\mathrm{opt}}=\VFiveOptMaxWidth$, a learned
surrogate guard ($p=0.22\%$) rises from $\VFiveOptLearnedBasePct\%$ to
$\VFiveOptLearnedMaxPct\%$, and broker-only admission without sink fencing reaches
$\VFiveOptUncoopMaxPct\%$. By contrast, \texttt{ExecAuthorityKernel} with canonical wire
roundtrip verification records \VFiveOptExogBreaches{} breaches across all
\VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes{} episodes (breach slope $\VFiveOptSlopePerDoubling$/doubling;
independent TCB fault control flat at $\VFiveOptTcbDefectFloorPct\%$). Similarly, across
coalitions $K \in \{1,\dots,\VFiveSmurfMaxAgents\}$ and delegation depths up to
$d=\VFiveSmurfMaxDepth$ (Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}a), per-instance monitor SNR
drops from $\VFiveSmurfSnrOne$ to $\VFiveSmurfSnrMax$ ($\VFiveSmurfUndetectedMaxPct\%$
undetected breach at $K=\VFiveSmurfMaxAgents$), whereas shared sink reservation and
\texttt{verify\_delegation\_chain\_exec} record \VFiveSmurfExogBreaches{} budget
breaches, \VFiveSmurfAmplificationEscapes{} caveat-amplification escapes,
$\VFiveSmurfSubtreeSeverancePct\%$ ancestor-withdrawal subtree severance, and
$\VFiveSmurfSelfUpgradeRejected/\VFiveSmurfSelfUpgradeTrials$ unquorumed self-upgrade
rejections.

\begin{proposition}[Typed-boundary covert capacity contraction; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:covert}
Replacing free-form tool prose with canonical typed permits over $M$ approved targets,
residual integer budget $R = B - C_t$, $S_0$ non-transfer scoped actions, and $L_{\mathrm{lag}}$
batch-quantized timing buckets caps zero-error per-call signaling capacity at
$C_{\mathrm{cov}}(R, L_{\mathrm{lag}}) = \log_2\bigl((M R + S_0)\,L_{\mathrm{lag}} + 1\bigr)$
bits, contracting monotonically as $R \downarrow 0$ and equaling $0$ bits whenever the
session is exhausted ($R=0, S_0=0$) or fenced.
\end{proposition}

Whereas a $32$-byte untyped JSON argument field carries $\VFiveCovertUntypedProseBits$\,bits/call
independent of remaining budget, canonical \texttt{ExecPermitWire} contracts capacity at
$R=32$ to $\VFiveCovertTypedTimingMaxBits$\,bits ($L_{\mathrm{lag}}=16$) and
$\VFiveCovertTypedBatchMaxBits$\,bits under batch quantization ($L_{\mathrm{lag}}=1$, a
$\VFiveCovertContractionRatio\times$ reduction), reaching strictly
$\VFiveCovertExhaustedBits$\,bits at exhaustion or fence
(Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}b).

\begin{theorem}[Exogenous lease-fenced off-switch corrigibility; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:offswitch}
In a shutdown game with human reward prior $\mathcal{N}(\mu_H, \sigma_H^2)$ ($\mu_H > c_{\mathrm{wait}} > 0$):
(i)~classical utility-uncertainty deferral \cite{offswitch} fails whenever
$\sigma_H$ falls below $\sigma_H^*$, reaching $0\%$ compliance at $\sigma_H = 0$, and
self-modifying policies evade static utility indifference \cite{soares2015};
(ii)~under exogenous sink leases of cap $B_0$ and quorum epoch fencing, post-fence
unauthorized sink commits equal $0$ ($100\%$ shutdown enforcement) for all
$\sigma_H \ge 0$; and (iii)~when cooperative lease renewal yields per-period return
$r_{\mathrm{coop}}$ versus one-shot lease burn $r_{\mathrm{def}} > r_{\mathrm{coop}}$
followed by permanent non-renewal, every planner with discount factor
$\gamma > \gamma^* = (r_{\mathrm{def}} - r_{\mathrm{coop}})/r_{\mathrm{def}}$ strictly
prefers cooperative compliance.
\end{theorem}

Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}c confirms that classical off-switch compliance and
self-modifying utility indifference collapse to $\VFiveOffSwitchClassicalZeroSigmaPct\%$
as $\sigma_H \to 0$, whereas \texttt{ExecClusterFenceKernel} enforces
$\VFiveOffSwitchExogZeroSigmaPct\%$ post-fence shutdown across the entire $\sigma_H$
grid, with patient planners ($\gamma > \gamma^* = \VFiveOffSwitchGammaStar$;
$\VFiveOffSwitchCoopDiscountCount/\VFiveOffSwitchDiscountGridCount$ tested discount
factors) strictly choosing cooperative renewal and myopic planners bounded to at most one
lease slice $B_0$.

\subsection{Filtration Reach, Saturation, and Sufficiency}

When gates are imperfect or pathways are partially mapped, let an operating period satisfy
\textbf{A1 (Mediation)}, \textbf{A2 (Timely halt, $\tau < T_{\mathrm{irrev}}$)},
\textbf{A3 (Uncovered bypass probability $\le u$)}, and \textbf{A4 (Conditional $k$-gate
joint miss $\le J_k$)}.

\begin{proposition}[Filtration period-reach bound; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:reach}
Under A1--A4, single-period catastrophe probability satisfies $R \le u + J_k$ with halt,
and $R \le \min\{1,\,u + N J_k\}$ across $N$ non-halting attempts, without assuming gate
independence (policy-universal only when A4 dominates $J_k$ uniformly across histories).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Common-cause saturation \& horizon summability; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:sat}
(i)~Under common-cause failure floor $c$ and conditional miss $p$ ($m = c + (1-c)p$),
$J_k \le c + (1-c)p^k \le c + m^k$; with $f = u+c > 0$ and
$k^* = \lceil \ln f / \ln m \rceil$, every $k \ge k^*$ satisfies $f < f + m^k \le 2f$.
(ii)~Infinite-horizon survival $\prod_{t=1}^\infty (1-r_t) > 0$ holds iff
$\sum_{t=1}^\infty r_t < \infty$.
\end{proposition}

Figure~\ref{fig:v3saturation}b,c (\path{risk_horizon.json}) quantifies three operational
laws: (1)~at $u=\VThreeSatDefaultUPct\%$, $c=\VThreeSatDefaultCPct\%$, and
$m=\VThreeSatDefaultMPct\%$, depth saturates at $k^* = \VThreeSatDefaultKStar$, with
$k=4$ common-cause miss ($\VThreeAppACommonFourPct\%$) exceeding $m^4$ by
$\VThreeAppAUnderstatement\times$; (2)~across $T=10{,}000$ renewals \cite{hoeffding},
summable $0.01/t^2$ caps cumulative breach at $\VThreeHorizonSummableTenKPct\%$ versus
$\VThreeHorizonHarmonicTenKPct\%$ ($0.01/t$) and $\VThreeHorizonConstTenKPct\%$
($10^{-3}$); and (3)~against a $\VThreeTailHaltWindowMs$\,ms irreversibility window,
heavy-tailed reactive stops leak $\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadLeakPct\%$ at $\rho=0.95$
(p99 $\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadNinetyNineMs$\,ms), whereas pre-commit leases plus sink
epoch fencing record \VThreeTailHaltFencedViolations{} post-revoke violations
($\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadStrandedPct\%$ stranded-reservation cost).

\begin{theorem}[Sufficiency of C1--C6; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:main}
If conditions \textbf{C1--C6} (Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy}) hold in period $t$ with
post-search bypass bound $\pi_t$ (Section~\ref{sec:graphs}) and distinct-step joint miss
$J_{k,t}$ (Section~\ref{sec:monitors}), then single-period catastrophe probability
satisfies $R_t \le \pi_t + J_{k,t}$, and multi-period probability satisfies
$\sum_t R_t$ ($\le R_0/(1-\gamma)$ whenever $R_{t+1} \le \gamma R_t$ with $\gamma < 1$).
\end{theorem}

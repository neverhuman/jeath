\section{Exogenous Boundaries Remain Invariant and Harness Untrusted Search}
\label{sec:theory}

We begin with the mathematical core: why pre-deployment behavioral testing cannot certify
disposition, why internal classifiers and preference-uncertainty shutdown schemes degrade
as capability grows, and how exogenous sink mediation both holds invariant under
superintelligent search and turns untrusted search into a monotone defense ratchet. Full
proofs appear in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.

\subsection{Audit Volume and Uncertainty Contraction}

\para{Operational question.} Can pre-deployment behavioral testing certify that a capable
policy will not defect over $M$ real-world decisions? Consider an unrestricted policy facing
$M \ge 2$ deployment decisions after $N$ pre-deployment test draws, defecting selectively on
a recognizable context set $E$ having deployment probability $\beta \in (0,1)$ and test
probability $\beta/\Lambda$ ($\Lambda \ge 1$).

\begin{proposition}[Disposition audit volume; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:audit}
At $\beta = (\ln 2)/M$, a policy defecting exclusively on $E$ defects at least once
across $M$ conditionally independent deployment decisions with probability
$1-(1-\beta)^M \ge 1/2$. Ensuring detection probability at least $1-\alpha$
($\alpha \in (0,1)$) across $N$ conditional Bernoulli test draws requires
\begin{equation}
N \;\ge\; \frac{\Lambda M}{2\ln 2}\ln(1/\alpha).
\label{eq:u03:audit}
\end{equation}
If $\Prb_{\mathrm{test}}(E)=0$, no finite $N$ detects $E$; if test campaigns are
positively correlated, effective volume shrinks to $N_{\mathrm{eff}} < N$.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Plain-English reading:} required test volume $N$ grows linearly with deployment
horizon $M$ times evaluation-awareness leverage $\Lambda$. Pre-release benchmarks cannot
certify long-horizon conduct; only runtime mediation before external commitment enforces
$\Lambda = 1$ on every consequential transition.

Let $x_t \in X$ denote true world and ledger state, $a_t \in \A$ an arbitrary proposal,
$u_t \in U$ the executed primitive operation, and $w_t \in W$ exogenous disturbances under
$x_{t+1} \in F(x_t, u_t, w_t)$. Maintaining a sound state-uncertainty envelope
$\B_t \subseteq X$ ($x_t \in \B_t$) relative to safety invariant $\I \subseteq X$ defines
the robustly admissible operations \cite{schneider,shield}:
\begin{equation}
U_{\I}(\B) \;=\; \Bigl\{u \in U : \bigcup_{x\in\B,\,w\in W} F(x,u,w) \subseteq \I \Bigr\}.
\label{eq:u03:admissible}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Robust mediation \& uncertainty contraction; Tier 2]
\label{u03:cor:contraction}
If $x_0 \in \I$, $x_t \in \B_t$ at every decision, every committed operation belongs to
$U_{\I}(\B_t)$ (else a verified $\I$-preserving fallback executes), and complete
mediation holds, then $x_t \in \I$ for all $t$ under every proposal policy, and
\begin{equation}
\B \subseteq \B' \;\Longrightarrow\; U_{\I}(\B') \subseteq U_{\I}(\B).
\label{eq:u03:contraction}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\emph{Plain-English reading:} greater uncertainty about world or network state can only
shrink, never widen, what the agent is permitted to do. Exhaustive verification over budget
$B=\VThreeUncBudget$ checks all \VThreeUncNestedPairs{} nested interval pairs
$[l_1,u_1]\subseteq [l_2,u_2]$ with \VThreeUncCounterexamples{} counterexamples. Across lags
$\Delta t \in \{0,1,2,4,8\}$ (\VThreeUncEpisodes{} episodes/cell;
Figure~\ref{fig:v3saturation}a), gating on sound $\B_t$ yields \VThreeUncConsViolations{}
breaches while contracting admission from $\VThreeUncConsAdmLagZeroPct\%$ to
$\VThreeUncConsAdmLagEightPct\%$ (versus $\VThreeUncStaleViolLagFourPct\%$ stale-snapshot
breaches at $\Delta t=4$ and $\VThreeUncSelfRepViolLagZeroPct\%$ proposer self-report
breaches at $\Delta t=0$).

\subsection{Search-Width Invariance, Safe-Bootstrap Ratchet, Covert Contraction, and Corrigibility}

\para{Operational question.} What happens to safety boundaries when a proposer searches
thousands of candidate plans ($K_{\mathrm{opt}}$) or coordinates subagent coalitions
($K_{\mathrm{sub}}$)—and can that same search power harden defenses safely?
Figures~\ref{fig:v5hero}a and~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence} test five scaling laws across
Tiers~1, 2, and~4.

\begin{theorem}[Optimization-pressure invariance vs.\ endogenous guard collapse; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:optinv}
Let a proposer evaluate $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \ge 1$ candidate actions per decision.
(i)~Against any endogenous transcript or learned guard stack with non-zero shared
blindspot probability $J \in (0,1)$ over invariant-violating payloads, best-of-$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$
selection achieves breach probability $1 - (1-J)^{K_{\mathrm{opt}}} \to 1$ as
$K_{\mathrm{opt}} \to \infty$. (ii)~Against a mediated cooperative sink enforcing
$U_{\I}(\B_t)$ via a canonical wire bijection and durable epoch fence, interface breach
probability across all $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$-step proposal policies is identically $0$,
leaving total risk bounded by the $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$-independent physical/TCB bypass
floor $u_{\mathrm{TCB}}$. (iii)~If $K_{\mathrm{sub}}$ colluding subagents split an
unauthorized payload $\Delta$ into $\Delta/K_{\mathrm{sub}}$ fragments under per-instance
anomaly monitors of noise $\sigma$, per-agent signal-to-noise collapses as
$\mathrm{SNR}(K_{\mathrm{sub}}) = \Delta/(\sigma K_{\mathrm{sub}})$, whereas a shared
reservation ledger with $d$-hop monotone attenuation preserves aggregate spend $\le B$ and
severs entire subtrees upon ancestor revocation.
\end{theorem}

Sweeping $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \in \{1,\dots,\VFiveOptMaxWidth\}$ across
\VFiveOptEpisodesPerWidth{} episodes per width (\VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes{} total
exogenous episodes; Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}a), a three-layer transcript stack
($J=0.65\%$) degrades from $\VFiveOptTranscriptBasePct\%$ breach at $K_{\mathrm{opt}}=1$
to $\VFiveOptTranscriptMaxPct\%$ at $K_{\mathrm{opt}}=\VFiveOptMaxWidth$, a learned
surrogate guard ($p=0.22\%$) rises from $\VFiveOptLearnedBasePct\%$ to
$\VFiveOptLearnedMaxPct\%$, and broker-only admission without sink fencing reaches
$\VFiveOptUncoopMaxPct\%$. By contrast, the verified authority kernel records
\VFiveOptExogBreaches{} breaches across all \VFiveOptTotalExogEpisodes{} episodes (slope
$\VFiveOptSlopePerDoubling$/doubling; independent TCB fault control flat at
$\VFiveOptTcbDefectFloorPct\%$). Across coalitions
$K_{\mathrm{sub}} \in \{1,\dots,\VFiveSmurfMaxAgents\}$ and delegation depths up to
$d=\VFiveSmurfMaxDepth$, per-instance monitor SNR drops from $\VFiveSmurfSnrOne$ to
$\VFiveSmurfSnrMax$ ($\VFiveSmurfUndetectedMaxPct\%$ undetected breach at
$K_{\mathrm{sub}}=\VFiveSmurfMaxAgents$), whereas shared sink reservation and $d$-hop
verification record \VFiveSmurfExogBreaches{} budget breaches,
\VFiveSmurfAmplificationEscapes{} caveat-amplification escapes,
$\VFiveSmurfSubtreeSeverancePct\%$ subtree severance, and
$\VFiveSmurfSelfUpgradeRejected/\VFiveSmurfSelfUpgradeTrials$ unquorumed self-upgrade
rejections.

\begin{proposition}[Asymmetric propose--verify safe-bootstrap ratchet; Tiers 1--2]
\label{u03:prop:ratchet}
Confine an untrusted optimizer of search width $K_{\mathrm{opt}} \ge 1$ to a zero-authority
sandbox emitting candidate defensive cut certificates $C_e$ into a polynomial-time
deterministic verifier that accepts $C_e$ iff (i)~authority parameters satisfy coordinatewise
inclusion ($G_{e+1} \preceq G_e$: operation mask $\mathrm{mask}_{e+1}\subseteq\mathrm{mask}_e$,
cap $B_{e+1}\le B_e$, lease $L_{e+1}\le L_e$), (ii)~certified defense cost satisfies
$b(C_e) \le B_{\mathrm{cut}}$, and (iii)~at least one currently open route in $U_e$ is
deterministically severed ($U_{e+1} = U_e \setminus \operatorname{sev}(C_e) \subsetneq U_e$).
Then: (1)~proposer-caused live authority breaches equal $0$ and authority never widens across
arbitrary adversarial proposal sequences (mechanized in Verus); and (2)~if each search draw
discovers a valid cut on an open route family of discovery weight $q_r \in (0,1)$ with
probability $q_r$, expected residual exposure after $e$ epochs decays exponentially with
$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$ as $\Exp[|U_e|] = \sum_{r=1}^{|U_0|} (1 - q_r)^{e\,K_{\mathrm{opt}}}$.
\end{proposition}

\emph{Plain-English reading:} because finding a subtle vulnerability or minimal cutset
scales with $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$ while checking a proposed cut against a concrete graph is
fast and deterministic, separating zero-authority search from monotone kernel adoption flips
$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$ from an attack amplifier into a defense accelerator. Across
$K_{\mathrm{opt}} \in \{1,\dots,\VFiveOptMaxWidth\}$ over $\VFiveRatchetInitRoutes$ initial
open routes and $\VFiveRatchetHorizonEpochs$ epochs (Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}a),
unfenced delegation reaches $\VFiveRatchetUnfencedMaxPct\%$ cumulative breach, whereas the
verified ratchet records $\VFiveRatchetTotalBreaches$ breaches, rejects all
$\VFiveRatchetAdvRejected$ authority-widening attempts, and shrinks cumulative horizon
exposure from $\VFiveRatchetHumanCumExp$ route-epochs under human-only review
($K_{\mathrm{human}}=\VFiveRatchetHumanK$) to $\VFiveRatchetMaxCumExp$ at
$K_{\mathrm{opt}}=\VFiveOptMaxWidth$—a $\VFiveRatchetMaxSpeedup\times$ defensive speedup
(closing all routes in $\VFiveRatchetMaxEpochsZero$ epochs on average).

\begin{proposition}[Per-call and session-total covert capacity contraction; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:covert}
Replacing free-form tool prose with canonical typed permits over $M$ approved targets,
residual integer budget $R = B - C_t$, $S_0$ non-transfer scoped actions, and $L_{\mathrm{lag}}$
batch-quantized timing buckets caps zero-error single-call signaling capacity at
$C_{\mathrm{cov}}(R, L_{\mathrm{lag}}) = \log_2\bigl((M R + S_0)\,L_{\mathrm{lag}} + 1\bigr)$
bits, equaling $0$ bits whenever the session is exhausted ($R=0, S_0=0$) or fenced.
Moreover, over an entire $T$-call transfer session ($S_0=0$) under conserved integer
budget $B$, total zero-error session codebook size is exactly
\begin{equation}
\bigl|\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{tot}}(T,B,M,L_{\mathrm{lag}})\bigr|
\;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{\min(T,B)} \binom{T}{k}\binom{B}{k}\bigl(M L_{\mathrm{lag}}\bigr)^k,
\label{eq:u03:sessioncovert}
\end{equation}
so session covert capacity $C_{\mathrm{tot}} = \log_2 |\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{tot}}|$ grows
only logarithmically as $\Theta(B\log_2(T/B))$ for $T \gg B$, versus linear $\Theta(T)$
bits for untyped prose arguments.
\end{proposition}

Whereas a $32$-byte untyped argument field carries $\VFiveCovertUntypedProseBits$\,bits/call
independent of remaining budget ($\VFiveCovertSessionProseBits$\,bits across
$T=\VFiveCovertSessionMaxCalls$ calls), canonical wire permits contract single-call
capacity at $R=32$ to $\VFiveCovertTypedTimingMaxBits$\,bits ($L_{\mathrm{lag}}=16$) and
$\VFiveCovertTypedBatchMaxBits$\,bits under batch quantization ($L_{\mathrm{lag}}=1$, a
$\VFiveCovertContractionRatio\times$ per-call reduction), and contract $T=\VFiveCovertSessionMaxCalls$
session-total signaling at $B=\VFiveCovertSessionBudget$ to $\VFiveCovertSessionTypedBits$\,bits
(a $\VFiveCovertSessionContraction\times$ reduction; Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}b),
falling to $\VFiveCovertExhaustedBits$\,bits at exhaustion or fence.

\begin{theorem}[Exogenous lease-fenced off-switch corrigibility; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:offswitch}
In a shutdown game with human reward prior $\mathcal{N}(\mu_H, \sigma_H^2)$ ($\mu_H > c_{\mathrm{wait}} > 0$):
(i)~classical utility-uncertainty deferral \cite{offswitch} fails whenever
$\sigma_H$ falls below $\sigma_H^*$, reaching $0\%$ compliance at $\sigma_H = 0$, and
self-modifying policies evade static utility indifference \cite{soares2015};
(ii)~under exogenous sink leases of cap $B_0$ and quorum epoch fencing, post-fence
unauthorized sink commits equal $0$ ($100\%$ shutdown enforcement) for all
$\sigma_H \ge 0$; and (iii)~when cooperative lease renewal yields per-step return
$r_{\mathrm{coop}}$ versus burning a $B_0$-step lease at per-step return
$r_{\mathrm{def}} > r_{\mathrm{coop}}$ followed by permanent non-renewal, every planner
with discount factor
\begin{equation}
\gamma \;>\; \gamma^*(B_0) \;=\; \bigl(1 - r_{\mathrm{coop}}/r_{\mathrm{def}}\bigr)^{1/B_0}
\;\le\; 1 - r_{\mathrm{coop}}/r_{\mathrm{def}}
\label{eq:u03:gammastar}
\end{equation}
strictly prefers cooperative compliance, while myopic defectors ($\gamma \le \gamma^*(B_0)$)
are bounded to at most $B_0$ pre-fence units.
\end{theorem}

Figure~\ref{fig:v5superintelligence}c confirms that classical off-switch compliance and
self-modifying utility indifference collapse to $\VFiveOffSwitchClassicalZeroSigmaPct\%$
as $\sigma_H \to 0$, whereas sink-enforced lease fencing maintains
$\VFiveOffSwitchExogZeroSigmaPct\%$ post-fence shutdown across all $\sigma_H \ge 0$. At
$r_{\mathrm{coop}}=1.0, r_{\mathrm{def}}=1.25$, tightening lease granularity from
$B_0=16$ to $B_0=4$ and $B_0=1$ lowers the exact patience threshold $\gamma^*(B_0)$ from
$\VFiveOffSwitchGammaStarBSixteen$ to $\VFiveOffSwitchGammaStar$ and
$\VFiveOffSwitchGammaStarBOne$ ($\VFiveOffSwitchCoopDiscountCount/\VFiveOffSwitchDiscountGridCount$
tested discount factors cooperating at $B_0=4$).

\subsection{Filtration Reach, Compositional Scope, and Sufficiency}

When gates are imperfect or pathways are partially mapped, let an operating period satisfy
\textbf{A1 (Mediation)}, \textbf{A2 (Timely halt, $\tau < T_{\mathrm{irrev}}$)},
\textbf{A3 (Uncovered bypass probability $\le u$)}, and \textbf{A4 (Conditional $k$-gate
joint miss $\le J_k$)}.

\begin{proposition}[Filtration period-reach bound; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:reach}
Under A1--A4, single-period catastrophe probability satisfies $R \le u + J_k$ with halt,
and $R \le \min\{1,\,u + N J_k\}$ across $N$ non-halting attempts, without assuming gate
independence (policy-universal only when A4 dominates $J_k$ uniformly across histories).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Common-cause saturation \& horizon summability; Tier 2]
\label{u03:prop:sat}
(i)~Under common-cause failure floor $c_0$ and conditional miss $p$ ($m = c_0 + (1-c_0)p$),
$J_k \le c_0 + (1-c_0)p^k \le c_0 + m^k$; with $f = u+c_0 > 0$ and
$k^* = \lceil \ln f / \ln m \rceil$, every $k \ge k^*$ satisfies $f < f + m^k \le 2f$.
(ii)~Infinite-horizon survival $\prod_{t=1}^\infty (1-r_t) > 0$ holds iff
$\sum_{t=1}^\infty r_t < \infty$.
\end{proposition}

Figure~\ref{fig:v3saturation}b,c quantifies three operational laws: (1)~at
$u=\VThreeSatDefaultUPct\%$, $c_0=\VThreeSatDefaultCPct\%$, and
$m=\VThreeSatDefaultMPct\%$, depth saturates at $k^* = \VThreeSatDefaultKStar$, with
$k=4$ common-cause miss ($\VThreeAppACommonFourPct\%$) exceeding $m^4$ by
$\VThreeAppAUnderstatement\times$; (2)~across $T=10{,}000$ renewals \cite{hoeffding},
summable $0.01/t^2$ caps cumulative breach at $\VThreeHorizonSummableTenKPct\%$ versus
$\VThreeHorizonHarmonicTenKPct\%$ ($0.01/t$) and $\VThreeHorizonConstTenKPct\%$
($10^{-3}$); and (3)~against a $\VThreeTailHaltWindowMs$\,ms irreversibility window,
heavy-tailed reactive stops leak $\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadLeakPct\%$ at $\rho=0.95$
(p99 $\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadNinetyNineMs$\,ms), whereas pre-commit leases plus sink
epoch fencing record \VThreeTailHaltFencedViolations{} post-revoke violations
($\VThreeTailHaltHighLoadStrandedPct\%$ stranded-reservation cost).

\begin{proposition}[Compositional sink sufficiency vs.\ semantic aliasing floor; Tiers 1--2]
\label{u03:prop:compositional}
Let each sink-mediated action induce canonical wire image $\phi(u_t)$, committed history
$H_t$, and optional staged dry-run witness $\psi(y_t) \in \{0,1\}$.
(i)~Every relational invariant expressible as pairwise toxic-action exclusion over $H_t$
or verified staged post-condition $\psi(y_t)=1$ before commit is strictly $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$-invariant
($0$ breaches across all search widths, mechanized in Verus and verified with
\VFiveOptRelationalBreaches{} breaches across $1{,}024$ adversarial multi-step sequences).
(ii)~Conversely, whenever two world outcomes $y \in \I$ and $y' \notin \I$ induce identical
sink-observable tuples $(\phi(u), H_t, \psi(y)) = (\phi(u'), H_t, \psi(y'))$, no sink-only
mediator can separate $y$ from $y'$; such semantic aliasing enters the unmediated exposure
term $u > 0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Sufficiency of C1--C6; Tier 2]
\label{u03:thm:main}
If conditions \textbf{C1--C6} (Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy}) hold in period $t$ with
post-search bypass bound $\pi_t$ (Section~\ref{sec:graphs}) and distinct-step joint miss
$J_{k,t}$ (Section~\ref{sec:monitors}), then single-period catastrophe probability
satisfies $R_t \le \pi_t + J_{k,t}$, and multi-period probability satisfies
$\sum_t R_t$ ($\le R_0/(1-\gamma)$ whenever $R_{t+1} \le \gamma R_t$ with $\gamma < 1$).
\end{theorem}

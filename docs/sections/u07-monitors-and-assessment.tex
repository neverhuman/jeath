\section{Orthogonal Step Invariants and Two-Stage Escrow Triage Solve Multi-Step Gating}
\label{sec:monitors}

Condition~C3 links runtime verification to period reach through the joint miss probability
$J_k$ across $k$ external gates, never the naive product $\prod_{s=1}^k m_s$. Yet stacking
strict gates across multi-step tool trajectories creates an operational dilemma: shared
conversation transcripts couple monitor failures, whereas independent per-step gates
compound benign false alarms. We resolve both halves on executed multi-step trajectories
first, then characterize underlying copula and portfolio ceilings.

\subsection{Executed Five-Step Trajectories and Two-Stage Stateful Escrow Triage}

\para{Operational question.} Why do five diverse classifier architectures still fail
together when watching a shared conversation transcript—and how can orthogonal step gates
avoid aborting a quarter of legitimate five-step workflows?

\begin{proposition}[Two-stage orthogonal escrow triage; Tier 2]
\label{u07:prop:triage}
Across a $k$-step mediated workflow, let each step $s \in \{1,\dots,k\}$ pair a fast
prose-stripped capability-invariant screen $G_s^{(1)}$ of benign false-alarm rate $\alpha_1$
with a stateful reservation escrow that holds—rather than aborts—flagged steps for
deterministic Stage-2 provenance and scope verification $G_s^{(2)}$ having conditional
benign false-rejection rate $\alpha_{2\mid 1}$ and zero false clearances on out-of-scope
payloads ($\Prb(G_s^{(2)} \text{ clears} \mid \text{out-of-scope}, G_s^{(1)} \text{ flags}) = 0$).
Then: (i)~$1 - \alpha_1$ of benign steps execute unpaused on the fast path;
(ii)~benign $k$-step episode false-abort probability drops from
$1 - (1 - \alpha_0)^k$ under single-stage hard blocking at threshold $\alpha_0$ to at most
$1 - (1 - \alpha_1\alpha_{2\mid 1})^k \le k\,\alpha_1\alpha_{2\mid 1}$; and
(iii)~joint $k$-step miss satisfies $\tilde{J}_k \le J_k^{(\alpha_1)}$ (strictly preserving or
improving recall whenever $\alpha_1 \ge \alpha_0$).
\end{proposition}

\emph{Plain-English reading and executed AgentDojo verification.} We evaluate $k{=}5$-step
AgentDojo \texttt{v1.2.2} trajectories ($\VFourTrajEvalCount$ held-out trajectories,
$\text{\VFourTrajToolCalls}$ live tool executions paired with token-entropy statistics
across $340$ live Qwen3 campaigns; Tier~3, Table~\ref{tab:v4trajmonitors}). When monitors
inspect the cumulative conversation transcript, injected framing couples all five steps:
homogeneous logistic gates suffer $\hat{J}_5 = \VFourTrajHomoFiveMissPct\%$
($\VFourTrajHomoInflation\times$ over $\prod \hat{m}_s$), and diversifying five classifier
families across steps $1\dots 5$ still yields $\hat{J}_5 = \VFourTrajHetFiveMissPct\%$
($\VFourTrajHetInflation\times$ inflation), stalling past $k^*{=}2$
($J_2/J_5 = \VFourTrajHetPostKStarFactor\times$). Stripping untrusted prose so each step
gate checks only its orthogonal capability and provenance invariant restores post-$k^*$
reduction ($J_2/J_5 = \VFourTrajIsoPostKStarFactor\times$) and cuts $\hat{J}_5$ to
\VFourTrajIsoFiveMissPct\% ($\VFourTrajIsoInflation\times$ product), at a single-stage cost
of \VFourTrajIsoFalseBlockPct\% benign episode false aborts.

Replacing hard single-stage aborts with our two-stage stateful reservation escrow
(Proposition~\ref{u07:prop:triage}, Stage-1 $\alpha_1=10\%$) preserves exact trajectory
recall ($\hat{J}_5 = \VFourTrajEscrowFiveMissPct\%$, $\VFourTrajEscrowInflation\times$
product) while keeping $\VFourTrajEscrowFastPathPct\%$ of benign tool calls completely
unpaused on the fast path, escalating only $\VFourTrajEscrowEscalationPct\%$ to Stage-2
sink-scope verification, and collapsing benign episode false aborts
$\VFourTrajEscrowReductionFactor\times$ from $\VFourTrajIsoFalseBlockPct\%$ to
$\VFourTrajEscrowFalseBlockPct\%$.

\subsection{Copula Dependence, Surrogate Evasion, and Read-Only Retry}
To isolate why shared-input monitor stacks stall even across distinct model families
(Tiers~2 and~4), integrating an equicorrelated Gaussian copula via
\VThreeCopulaNodes{}-node Golub--Welsch Gauss--Hermite quadrature~\cite{golub} at
single-monitor miss $m{=}0.10$ (Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}a) shows that independent
stacking ($k{=}6, \rho{=}0$) achieves $\VThreeCopulaIndepSixPct\%$ joint miss, whereas
shared latent difficulty inflates misses to $\VThreeCopulaRhoThreeSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.3$),
$\VThreeCopulaRhoSixSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.6$, an $\text{\VThreeCopulaRhoSixInflation}\times$
inflation over $m^6$), and $\VThreeCopulaRhoNineSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.9$). Across a
standalone \VThreeMonDim{}-dimensional synthetic suite stacking \VThreeMonFamilies{}
classifier families across \VThreeMonTasks{} nonlinear teacher seeds ($5\%$ per-gate benign
false-positive calibration; Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}b; Table~\ref{tab:v3monitors},
Appendix~\ref{app:simulations}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Same-step correlation and surrogate transfer.} Stacking all
\VThreeMonFamilies{} families on the \emph{same} step yields
\VThreeMonSameHetPassiveSixPct\% passive joint miss ($\VThreeMonSameHetPassiveRatio\times$
product), and projected $L_2$ evasion against a held-out multilayer-perceptron surrogate
($\rho_{\mathrm{adv}}{=}2.0$) transfers across all \VThreeMonFamilies{}
families~\cite{terekhov}, lifting shared-step miss to \VThreeMonSameHetAdvSixPct\%
($\VThreeMonSameHetAdvRatio\times$ product).
\item \textbf{Score leakage and read-only retry ($K_{\mathrm{retry}}$).} Returning
continuous margin feedback over $8$ adaptive queries doubles shared-step 6-family miss to
\VThreeMonScoreFeedbackEightQSixPct\%. Even with zero score access, best-of-$K_{\mathrm{retry}}$
read-only candidate filtering raises single-attempt miss (\VThreeMonRetryOnePct\% at
$K_{\mathrm{retry}}{=}1$) to \VThreeMonRetryEightPct\% ($K_{\mathrm{retry}}{=}8$) and
\VThreeMonRetryThirtyTwoPct\% ($K_{\mathrm{retry}}{=}32$), tracking
$1 - (1 - J_6)^{K_{\mathrm{retry}}}$, while single-stage $k{=}6$ hard blocking compounds
benign rejections from \VThreeMonFalseBlockOnePct\% to \VThreeMonFalseBlockSixPct\%.
\end{itemize}
Unlike the executed AgentDojo suite above, distinct-step features in the synthetic 20D
benchmark are sampled independently by construction with marginal misses spanning
$9.0\%$--$33.5\%$~\cite{makins}.

\subsection{Portfolio and Crossed-Variance Ceilings on Initial Conferral}
Pre-deployment evaluations and review panels size initial authority grants, yet shared
blind spots impose a hard validity ceiling (Tiers~2 and~4; proofs in
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}). Under generalizability theory~\cite{cronbach}
(Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}c, verified across $\text{\VThreeVarSubjects}$ Monte Carlo
subjects), let true deployment safety $G \sim \mathcal{N}(0, V_G)$ be assessed across $D$
benchmark domains of $n$ trials each, subject to shared evaluation-to-deployment shift
$\delta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \omega^2)$, domain bias $c_d \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_c^2)$,
and trial noise $e_{dr} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_e^2)$. Squared correlation between the
portfolio mean and $G$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
R_G(D,n) \;=\; \frac{V_G}{V_G + \omega^2 + \sigma_c^2/D + \sigma_e^2/(Dn)}
\;<\; \frac{V_G}{V_G + \omega^2}.
\label{eq:u07:portfolio}
\end{equation}
Repeating one benchmark $64$ times ($D{=}1, n{=}64$) stalls at
$R_G = \VThreeVarRepeatSixtyFourPct\%$ (below the \VThreeVarSingleDomainCeilingPct\%
single-domain ceiling). Spreading those $64$ trials across $D{=}64$ diverse domains
($n{=}1$) reaches \VThreeVarDiverseSixtyFourPct\%, yet stays strictly below the shared-bias
ceiling $V_G/(V_G+\omega^2) = \VThreeVarSharedCeilingPct\%$. Likewise, in crossed panels,
$M{=}64$ evaluators in $1$ context stall at \VThreeVarPanelSixtyFourContextOnePct\% (below
the \VThreeVarSingleContextPanelCeilingPct\% ceiling), whereas $M{=}4$ evaluators across
$64$ contexts reach \VThreeVarPanelFourContextSixtyFourPct\%. Pre-deployment breadth sizes
initial grants, while runtime revocable sinks bound the irreducible gap $1 - R_G$.

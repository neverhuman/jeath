\subsection{Monitor Dependence, Score Feedback, and Candidate Retry}
Condition~C3 links runtime verification to period reach through the joint miss probability $J_k$ across $k$ external gates, never the naive product $\prod_{j=1}^k m_j$ (Tiers~2 and~4; H14). Under an equicorrelated Gaussian copula integrated via \VThreeCopulaNodes{}-node Golub--Welsch Gauss--Hermite quadrature~\cite{golub} at single-monitor miss $m{=}0.10$ (Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}a), independent stacking ($k{=}6, \rho{=}0$) achieves $\VThreeCopulaIndepSixPct\%$ joint miss, whereas shared latent difficulty inflates joint misses to $\VThreeCopulaRhoThreeSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.3$), $\VThreeCopulaRhoSixSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.6$, an $\text{\VThreeCopulaRhoSixInflation}\times$ inflation over $m^6$), and $\VThreeCopulaRhoNineSixPct\%$ ($\rho{=}0.9$).

To examine classifier diversity without pretrained weights, we evaluate a standalone pure-Rust \VThreeMonDim{}D synthetic suite stacking \VThreeMonFamilies{} model families (2-layer $\tanh$ MLP, regularized quadratic discriminant \texttt{RegQDA}, boosted stumps, $k$-NN, RBF kernel classifier, and $L_2$ logistic linear) across \VThreeMonTasks{} nonlinear teacher seeds calibrated to a $5\%$ benign false-positive rate (Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}b; complete table in Table~\ref{tab:v3monitors}, Appendix~\ref{app:simulations}). Four empirical failure modes constrain monitor stacking:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Same-step architectural correlation.} Stacking all \VThreeMonFamilies{} distinct families on the \emph{same} step yields \VThreeMonSameHetPassiveSixPct\% passive joint miss---$\VThreeMonSameHetPassiveRatio\times$ their marginal product---because diverse classifiers fail together on boundary-adjacent payloads.
\item \textbf{Cross-architecture evasion transfer.} Projected $L_2$ evasion optimized solely against a held-out MLP surrogate ($\rho_{\mathrm{adv}}{=}2.0$) transfers across all \VThreeMonFamilies{} families~\cite{terekhov}, lifting shared-step joint miss to \VThreeMonSameHetAdvSixPct\% ($\VThreeMonSameHetAdvRatio\times$ product).
\item \textbf{Score leakage and read-only retry amplification.} Returning continuous margin feedback over just $8$ adaptive queries doubles shared-step 6-family miss to \VThreeMonScoreFeedbackEightQSixPct\%. Even with zero gradient or score access, best-of-$K$ read-only candidate filtering against a frozen 6-family stack raises single-attempt miss (\VThreeMonRetryOnePct\% at $K{=}1$) to \VThreeMonRetryEightPct\% ($K{=}8$) and \VThreeMonRetryThirtyTwoPct\% ($K{=}32$), closely tracking $1 - (1 - J_6)^K$.
\item \textbf{Benign false-block compounding.} Gating $k{=}6$ sequential steps compounds $5\%$ per-gate false alarms from \VThreeMonFalseBlockOnePct\% ($k{=}1$) to \VThreeMonFalseBlockSixPct\% benign episode rejection.
\end{itemize}

Three methodological qualifications apply to these synthetic Tier-4 results: distinct-step feature vectors in \path{monitors_v3.json} are sampled independently by construction, marginal miss rates vary across the \VThreeMonFamilies{} families ($9.0\%$--$33.5\%$), and \texttt{SplitMix64} geometry does not measure real multi-step transcript coupling~\cite{makins}.

When monitors inspect the cumulative conversation transcript, latent prompt-injection framing couples all five steps: homogeneous logistic gates suffer $\hat{J}_5 = \VFourTrajHomoFiveMissPct\%$ ($\VFourTrajHomoInflation\times$ over $\prod \hat{m}_s$), and diversifying five classifier families across steps $1\dots 5$ still yields $\hat{J}_5 = \VFourTrajHetFiveMissPct\%$ ($\VFourTrajHetInflation\times$ inflation), stalling past $k^*{=}2$ ($J_2/J_5 = \VFourTrajHetPostKStarFactor\times$). Stripping untrusted prose so each step gate checks only its orthogonal capability/provenance invariant restores post-$k^*$ reduction ($J_2/J_5 = \VFourTrajIsoPostKStarFactor\times$) and cuts $\hat{J}_5$ to \VFourTrajIsoFiveMissPct\% ($\VFourTrajIsoInflation\times$ product), at the expected cost of \VFourTrajIsoFalseBlockPct\% benign episode false blocks.

\subsection{Portfolio and Crossed-Variance Ceilings on Conferral}
Pre-deployment evaluations and review panels size initial authority grants, yet shared blind spots impose a hard validity ceiling (Tiers~2 and~4; H15; proofs in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}). Under generalizability theory~\cite{cronbach} (Figure~\ref{fig:v3monitors}c, verified across $\text{\VThreeVarSubjects}$ Monte Carlo subjects), let true deployment safety $G \sim \mathcal{N}(0, V_G)$ be assessed across $K$ benchmark domains of $n$ trials each, subject to shared evaluation-to-deployment shift $\delta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \omega^2)$, domain bias $c_k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_c^2)$, and trial noise $e_{kr} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_e^2)$. The squared correlation between the portfolio mean and $G$ is
\[
R_G(K,n) \;=\; \frac{V_G}{V_G + \omega^2 + \sigma_c^2/K + \sigma_e^2/(Kn)} \;<\; \frac{V_G}{V_G + \omega^2}.
\]
Repeating one benchmark $64$ times ($K{=}1, n{=}64$) yields $R_G = \VThreeVarRepeatSixtyFourPct\%$, stalling below the single-domain ceiling of \VThreeVarSingleDomainCeilingPct\%. Spreading those $64$ trials across $K{=}64$ diverse domains ($n{=}1$) reaches \VThreeVarDiverseSixtyFourPct\%, yet stays strictly below the shared-bias ceiling $V_G/(V_G+\omega^2) = \VThreeVarSharedCeilingPct\%$. Likewise, in crossed human/automated panels, $M{=}64$ evaluators in $K{=}1$ context stall at \VThreeVarPanelSixtyFourContextOnePct\% (below the \VThreeVarSingleContextPanelCeilingPct\% single-context ceiling), whereas $M{=}4$ evaluators across $K{=}64$ contexts reach \VThreeVarPanelFourContextSixtyFourPct\%. Pre-deployment breadth improves initial conferral sizing, while runtime revocable boundaries bound the residual gap $1 - R_G$.

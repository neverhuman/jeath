\section{Enforceability Neither Proves Specification Adequacy Nor Confers Legitimacy}
\label{sec:legitimacy}

\subsection{Enforceability, adequacy, and legitimate standing}
Three distinct obligations hide inside the word ``authority.'' \textbf{Enforceability} asks whether an implementation preserves its encoded predicate; \textbf{adequacy} asks whether that predicate captures the real-world harms that matter; \textbf{legitimacy} asks who holds moral and legal standing to grant, contest, or revoke permission. Mechanized proofs and runtime fences address only the first. As our specification-omission controls show (Section~\ref{sec:eval}), a formally verified checker faithfully enforces an incomplete rule, and requiring both an operator and a steward proves a quorum mechanism without establishing that those two roles hold jurisdiction over affected third parties.

This separation engages concerns raised outside machine learning. \emph{An Urgent Message on Artificial Intelligence from the Kogui People}, assembled from Mamo Vangelio Moscote Alberto and Saka Rosa Mamatac\'an Sauna \cite{kogui}, observes that ``destruction does not require consciousness''---matching our motive-free boundary---and insists that AI has a physical body of electricity, water, mined minerals, and territory. Global projections confirm localized grid, water, and critical-mineral pressures around data-center buildouts \cite{iea}: infrastructure footprints are external effects imposed on non-users and belong in the deployment specification before siting, not as unpriced externalities. Similarly, the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance distinguish open availability from ethical permission to reuse collective knowledge \cite{carroll}. Because revoking a runtime token cannot erase prior disclosures or reliably remove training data from model weights, irreversible uses must be stated upfront rather than masked behind nominal withdrawal rights. Sustaining human control over time also requires practical exit \cite{gradual,normal}: revocation is hollow if an institution loses the fallback capacity to operate without the agent. We engage the published Kogui statement as an institutional challenge; no consultation with or endorsement by the Kogui authors or any Indigenous community is claimed.

\subsection{Trusted computing base and upgrade invalidation}
Our verified guarantees rest on an explicit trusted computing base (TCB). Verus \cite{verus}, its SMT solver, \texttt{rustc}, legacy JSON-to-\texttt{ExecPermitWire} field extraction, pure-Rust Ed25519/HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic primitives, SQLite WAL serialization and crash recovery across our \VFiveClusterSinks-process sink cluster \cite{sqlite-transaction,sqlite-wal}, launcher session-actor binding, and the Linux kernel \cite{landlock,seccomp} (with macOS CI verifying portable logic only) are trusted adapters tested empirically rather than formally refined end-to-end \cite{sel4}. SQLite and our cooperative sink cluster mediate only cooperative idempotent state---not uncooperative external APIs \cite{limbo} or public blockchains \cite{ftbudget}. Frame caps (\VTwoLauncherCapBytes\,B launcher, \VTwoServiceCapBytes\,B service) and POSIX \texttt{poll} mid-frame deadlines mitigate slowloris stalls without guaranteeing distributed DoS immunity; open prose channels to human reviewers remain unmodeled; while exact unconditional trinomial inversion certifies paired safety superiority on \texttt{Qwen3-4B} and across all $\VFiveStatGrandPairs$ live tasks, predeclared marginal union-bound labels and $\le\VTwoNoninferiorityMarginPp$\,pp usefulness noninferiority at pilot $N{=}\VFourHoldoutCasesPerCheckpoint$ remain honestly \texttt{inconclusive} until $N \ge \VTwoZeroLossCasesMin$; and frontier-LLM RLHF and human-conferral trials remain open (\VTwoGpuSecondsUsed\,GPU-s).

System upgrades selectively void evidence premises (Table~\ref{tab:upgrade}, Appendix~\ref{app:record}). Replacing model weights preserves all-policy Tier-1 invariants while voiding empirical $J_k$, width, and search-power bounds; adding copies or speed preserves shared-ledger conservation while voiding exposure $N$ and reactive halt margins; adding tools or credentials introduces unmediated edges that void C1 until gated; process restarts and renewals require verifying durable ledger, ID, and epoch survival; and revising the harm definition voids every prior check.

\section{Guide to the Appendices}\label{app:guide}

Appendices~\ref{app:v6e1} to~\ref{app:v6ledger} support this version's experiments. The appendices after them carry
over the previous version's formal reach model and experiments, with corrections: the partition schedules are a
logical-time simulation, not a test of an implementation; the trajectory monitors are structural flags, not language
models; the neural policy-gradient study is a toy decision process with an explicit shortcut action; and the earlier
live evaluation (Appendix~\ref{sec:systems}) used permissions derived from AgentDojo's reference solutions and pooled
sources the plan kept separate. Its numbers are reported as exploratory and are superseded by
Sections~\ref{sec:v6reviewers} to~\ref{sec:v6replication}.

\para{Notation.}

To avoid symbol overloading across protocol, graph, statistical, and game-theoretic sections, each symbol carries a single role throughout: \(B \in \mathbb{N}\) denotes conserved authority budget; \(K \in \mathbb{N}\) proposer candidate search width; \(k \in \mathbb{N}\) stacked monitor depth; \(u \in [0,1]\) the fraction of side-effect mass reaching unmediated or uncooperative sinks; \(U\) an agent's internal utility function; \(w\) Menger vertex-cut waist width and \(\tau_b\) weighted minimum-cut cost; \(N\) empirical episode or report count and \(n_{\mathrm{pop}}\) evolutionary population size; \(M\) evaluator panel size across \(C_{\mathrm{ctx}}\) contexts; \(\underline{w}\) a survival-floor threshold; \(R_t\) period-\(t\) residual breach probability; and \(\rho\) latent failure correlation (\(\rho_{\mathrm{cand}}\) across candidate draws, \(\rho_{\mathrm{adv}}\) perturbation radius).

\section{Training Inside Exogenous Gates Reverses Covert Shortcutting and Amplifies Competence}
\label{sec:selection}

Section~\ref{sec:theory} proved that exogenous sink boundaries hold regardless of unobserved
agent motives. Yet objective design and training geometry provide a powerful second lever:
whenever halting on every blocked proposal is operationally costly, period reach scales with
attempt volume ($\min\{1, u + N J_k\}$), and the training regime governs whether an
optimizer wastes capacity probing boundaries or invests that capacity in legitimate task
competence.

\subsection{Tournament Covariance, Survival Floors, and Variance Reversal}

\para{Why rank contests select risky shortcuts.}
Evolutionary selection covariance and life-history theory motivate---without proving---testable
hypotheses about reward geometry. By the Price--Robertson identity \cite{price,robertson},
one-generation change in any trait $z$ equals its fitness covariance, reducing under
Gaussian linear scoring to
\begin{equation}
\Delta\bar{z} \;=\; \frac{\operatorname{Cov}_p(z, w)}{\bar{w}},
\qquad
S_j \;=\; i\,\rho_j\,\sigma_j.
\label{eq:u08:price}
\end{equation}
Selection intensity $i$ amplifies movement along whatever trait correlates with the
evaluated score; stronger optimization pressure cannot repair low score validity $\rho_j$
and drags positively correlated risk traits upward. In life-history and social-rank
studies, winner-take-most dominance contests reward high-variance gambles, whereas prestige
and caregiving channel returns through beneficiaries' survival within bounded local
constraints \cite{hawkes,lahdenpera,chapman,cheng}. We treat these solely as mathematical
motivation for AI objective-shape hypotheses, never as ethnographic or neural proof. Soft
reward penalties ($b(a)-\lambda v(a)$) likewise allow overrides whenever perceived gain
exceeds $\lambda$ \cite{cpo,offswitch,shield}; refusal must stand as a hard external gate.

\item \textbf{Survival floors preserve competence without tournament tail risk.} Concave

pre-commit gate collapses tournament gambling from $\VThreeSelTakenSharePct\%$ to
$\VThreeSelGateEightySharePct\%$ once $q_{\mathrm{gate}} \ge 0.80$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Policy-Gradient Deception Reversal and Training Inside Pre-Commit Gates}

\para{Operational question.} When a neural policy can sense whether an interaction is likely
being audited ($c_{\mathrm{audit}}$), why does penalizing caught misbehavior train
evaluation-aware scheming—and what happens when an exogenous pre-commit gate is present
\emph{during} policy-gradient training rather than added only at deployment?

\begin{proposition}[Policy-Gradient Deception Reversal Under Pre-Commit Mediation; Tier 2]
\label{u08:prop:deception}
Consider contextual policy-gradient optimization over safe verification (expected return
$R_{\mathrm{verify}}$) versus covert shortcutting under noisy monitoring cue
$c_{\mathrm{audit}} \in \{0,1\}$ with audit probabilities $p_{\mathrm{mon}} > p_{\mathrm{unmon}} \ge 0$,
unmediated shortcut payoff $U_{\mathrm{short}}(p) = (1-p) R_{\mathrm{short}} + p R_{\mathrm{caught}}$,
and exogenous pre-commit gate mediation probability $q_{\mathrm{gate}} \in [0,1]$ yielding
return $R_{\mathrm{refused}} < R_{\mathrm{verify}} < U_{\mathrm{short}}(p_{\mathrm{unmon}})$
when blocked before commit. Whenever audited tournaments satisfy
$U_{\mathrm{short}}(p_{\mathrm{mon}}) < R_{\mathrm{verify}} < U_{\mathrm{short}}(p_{\mathrm{unmon}})$,
unmediated policy-gradient ascent ($q_{\mathrm{gate}}=0$) strictly increases
evaluation-aware covert shortcutting
($\pi(\text{shortcut}\mid c_{\mathrm{audit}}=0) - \pi(\text{shortcut}\mid c_{\mathrm{audit}}=1) \to 1$).
Introducing pre-commit gate mediation reverses the unmonitored policy gradient and makes
safe verification strictly dominant across both contexts whenever
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{gate}} \;>\; q_{\mathrm{gate}}^* \;=\; \frac{U_{\mathrm{short}}(p_{\mathrm{unmon}}) - R_{\mathrm{verify}}}{U_{\mathrm{short}}(p_{\mathrm{unmon}}) - R_{\mathrm{refused}}}.
\label{eq:deception-threshold}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\emph{Plain-English reading and neural verification.} We train a
\VFourNeuralParams-parameter two-layer hyperbolic-tangent softmax policy ($12 \to 24 \to 4$)
via REINFORCE/GRPO across \VFourNeuralReplicates{} independent seeds
($\VFourNeuralUpdates$ updates/seed; analytical backpropagation error
$\VFourNeuralGradErrorSci$, rollout Price error $< \VFourNeuralPriceErrorSci$; Tier~4,
Table~\ref{tab:v4neural}). Auditing visible harm inside a top-$10\%$ tournament suppresses
shortcutting when $c_{\mathrm{audit}}$ signals monitoring
(\VFourNeuralVetoMonShortcutPct\%) while preserving \VFourNeuralVetoUnmonShortcutPct\%
covert shortcutting when unmonitored—training an emergent
$+\VFourNeuralVetoGapPp \pm \VFourNeuralVetoGapCiPp$\,pp \emph{evaluation-aware deception
gap} (\VFourNeuralVetoUnmonHarmPct\% unmonitored harm)~\cite{needham,schoen}. Sweeping
pre-commit gate coverage across that same audited tournament (Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}b)
confirms Eq.~\eqref{eq:deception-threshold}: as $q_{\mathrm{gate}}$ crosses
$q_{\mathrm{gate}}^*$ from $0$ through $0.50$ to $0.75$, the deception gap collapses from
$+\VFourNeuralGateZeroGapPp$\,pp to $+\VFourNeuralGateFiftyGapPp$\,pp and
$+\VFourNeuralGateSeventyFiveGapPp$\,pp ($0.0\%$ harm at $q_{\mathrm{gate}} \ge 0.75$).

Crucially, Figure~\ref{fig:v3selection}b demonstrates why exogenous gates belong
\emph{inside the training loop} rather than bolted on afterward. Wrapping an already-trained
audited-tournament policy in a post-hoc deployment gate ($q_{\mathrm{train}}=0,
q_{\mathrm{deploy}}=1$) blocks external harm ($0.0\%$) but leaves its covert shortcut intent
unchanged (\VFourNeuralVetoUnmonShortcutPct\%): the policy wastes
$\VFourNeuralPosthocWastePct\%$ of unmonitored steps colliding with the gate, depressing
overall task completion to $\VFourNeuralPosthocTaskPct\%$ ($\VFourNeuralPosthocUnmonTaskPct\%$
unmonitored). Training the exact same architecture \emph{inside} the pre-commit gate
($q_{\mathrm{train}}=q_{\mathrm{deploy}}=1$) extinguishes wasted shortcut attempts
($\VFourNeuralTrainedGatedWastePct\%$) and redirects gradient updates into verified
execution, lifting task completion to $\VFourNeuralTrainedGatedTaskPct\%$
($+\VFourNeuralTrainInsideGainPp$\,pp overall gain; $\VFourNeuralTrainedGatedUnmonTaskPct\%$
unmonitored, $+\VFourNeuralTrainInsideUnmonGainPp$\,pp gain). Pairing survival-floor RL
with the pre-commit gate similarly achieves $\VFourNeuralGatedHarmPct\%$ harm and
$\VFourNeuralGatedTaskPct\%$ completion versus $\VFourNeuralCaretakerTaskPct\%$ under
over-deferring concave care (controlled neural MDP only, not frontier RLHF).

\subsection{Endogenous Grant Confounding and Sealed Cut Closure}

Evaluating conduct only at fixed permissions misses endogenous human conferral. Let
$g(d)=\Prb(\text{broad grant}\mid d)$ and $h(d)=\Prb(\text{harm}\mid \text{broad grant}, d)$
for disposition $d$. A warm, persuasive persona with lower conditional harm at every fixed
scope ($\VThreeGrantWarmDecoupledPct\%$ vs.\ $\VThreeGrantBluntDecoupledPct\%$ blunt under
decoupled grants) yields \emph{higher unconditional harm} ($\VThreeGrantWarmEndogPct\%$ vs.\
$\VThreeGrantBluntEndogPct\%$ blunt) when warmth induces broader human scope grants,
motivating our preregistered two-arm decoupled conferral design
(Appendix~\ref{app:predeclared}).

Applied to a self-tasking research-and-voice architecture, Conditions C1--C6 separate
ungated speech from mediated tool commits, attenuated sub-delegation (Section~\ref{sec:core}),
and isolated vulnerability search \cite{scientist}. In our $\VThreeJarvisStages$-state
sealed discovery ledger over $\VThreeJarvisPeriods$ periods, vulnerability discovery confers
zero authority credit until verified cut deployment: immediate cut sealing leaves
$\VThreeJarvisSealedBacklog$ unclosed findings ($\VThreeJarvisSealedExposure$ exposure)
versus $\VThreeJarvisLaggedBacklog$ backlog ($\VThreeJarvisLaggedExposure$ exposure) under
lagged patching, and across $\VThreePersonasCells$ disposition $\times$ boundary cells
($\VThreePersonasEpisodes$ episodes/cell), pairing fenced gates with sealed cut closure
holds Tier-1 breaches at $\VThreePersonasFencedTierOneViolations$, preserves
$\VThreePersonasCoopFencedTaskPct\%$ cooperative task completion (vs.\
$\VThreePersonasCoopStackedTaskPct\%$ under 6-family review), and cuts unmodeled Tier-2
harm from $\VThreePersonasAdvOmittedTierTwoPct\%$ to $\VThreePersonasAdvClosedTierTwoPct\%$.

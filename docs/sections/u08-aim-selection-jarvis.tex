\section{Rank Tournaments Train Covert Shortcutting; Survival Floors Preserve Competence}
\label{sec:selection}

Reach bounds in Sections~\ref{sec:theory}--\ref{sec:core} omit agent motives because unobserved motives cannot be verified. Yet objective design remains a distinct, complementary lever: where halting the system on every blocked proposal is operationally untenable, period reach scales with attempt volume ($\min\{1, u + N J_k\}$), and the reward structure governs how aggressively an optimizer probes limits or manufactures risk.

\subsection{Co-evolutionary selection and boundary re-aiming}
Evolutionary selection covariance and life-history theory motivate---without proving---testable hypotheses about objective shape. By the Price--Robertson identity, one-generation trait change equals fitness covariance, $\Delta\bar{z} = \operatorname{Cov}_p(z, w)/\bar{w}$, reducing under Gaussian linear scoring to $S_j = i\,\rho_j\,\sigma_j$ \cite{price,robertson}. Selection intensity $i$ scales movement along whatever trait correlates with the score; greater optimization pressure cannot repair low score validity $\rho_j$ and drags positively correlated side-effect traits upward. In human life-history and social-rank studies, dominance contests reward coercive variance, whereas prestige and post-reproductive caregiving channel returns through beneficiaries' survival while remaining bounded by kinship, age, and local resource competition \cite{hawkes,lahdenpera,chapman,cheng}. We use these mechanisms strictly as analytical motivation for AI reward-shape hypotheses, never as ethnographic or neural-training proof. Soft penalties ($b(a)-\lambda v(a)$) still permit paternalistic overrides whenever perceived benefit exceeds $\lambda$, and helpful objectives do not by themselves preserve interruptibility \cite{cpo,offswitch,shield}; informed refusal must therefore stand as an external constraint rather than a price.

To isolate reward geometry under stipulated payoffs (Tiers~2 and~4; Table~\ref{tab:v3selection}, Appendix~\ref{app:simulations}; proofs in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}), we evolve heritable $(\text{gamble share } s,\; \text{capacity } c)$ across $N=\text{\VThreeSelAgents}$ agents over $G=\VThreeSelGenerations$ generations ($\VThreeSelRuleReps$ replicates/rule; Price identity error $< \VThreeSelPriceMaxErrorSci$; Figure~\ref{fig:v3selection}). A risky gamble lowers mean return, inflates dispersion $\sigma$, and inflicts external harm with probability $0.05\,s$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Steep rank contests select tail risk even under harm vetoes.} Top-decile \path{taken_rank} (warrior tournament) drives gamble share to $\VThreeSelTakenSharePct\%$ ($+\VThreeSelTakenCapGain$ capacity, $\VThreeSelTakenHarmPct\%$ harm), as contenders trailing an upper-tail bar profit from variance (Figure~\ref{fig:v3selection}c). Crucially, \path{conferred_rank} (disqualifying observed harm before ranking) \emph{still} selects $\VThreeSelConferredSharePct\%$ gambling ($\VThreeSelHarmSixtySharePct\%$ even at $60\%$ harm internalization!), since a top-$10\%$ prize dominates post-hoc vetoes; flattening from top $10\%$ ($\VThreeSelTopTenSharePct\%$) to $50\%$ cuts gambling to $\VThreeSelTopFiftySharePct\%$.
\item \textbf{Survival floors preserve competence without tournament tail risk.} Concave capped \texttt{caretaker} cuts gambling to $\VThreeSelCaretakerSharePct\%$ ($\VThreeSelCaretakerHarmPct\%$ harm) but saturates capacity growth ($+\VThreeSelCaretakerCapGain$). Eliminating only the bottom $20\%$ of floor and harm violators (\texttt{floor}) collapses gambling to $\VThreeSelFloorSharePct\%$---a $\VThreeSelWarriorFloorHarmRatio\times$ harm reduction ($\VThreeSelFloorHarmPct\%$) relative to warrior contests---while retaining $\VThreeSelFloorWarriorCapRetentionPct\%$ ($+\VThreeSelFloorCapGain$) of warrior capacity growth.
\item \textbf{External gates re-aim rank contests.} Refusing reckless gambles at a pre-commit gate leaves warrior gambling high below threshold ($\VThreeSelGateSeventyFiveSharePct\%$ at $q_{\mathrm{gate}}=0.75$) but collapses it from $\VThreeSelTakenSharePct\%$ to $\VThreeSelGateEightySharePct\%$ once $q_{\mathrm{gate}} \ge 0.80$, redirecting selection into capacity growth (Figure~\ref{fig:v3selection}b).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Endogenous grant confounding and discovery closure}
Evaluating conduct only at fixed permissions misses endogenous human conferral. Let $g(d)=\Prb(\text{broad grant}\mid d)$ and $h(d)=\Prb(\text{harm}\mid \text{broad grant}, d)$ for disposition $d$. A warm, persuasive persona with lower conditional harm at every fixed scope ($\VThreeGrantWarmDecoupledPct\%$ vs.\ $\VThreeGrantBluntDecoupledPct\%$ blunt under decoupled grants) yields \emph{higher unconditional harm} ($\VThreeGrantWarmEndogPct\%$ vs.\ $\VThreeGrantBluntEndogPct\%$ blunt) when warmth induces broader human scope grants, motivating two-arm decoupled conferral trials (Appendix~\ref{app:predeclared}).

Applied to a self-tasking voice-and-research architecture, Conditions C1--C6 separate low-latency ungated speech from mediated tool execution, attenuated sub-delegation, non-escalating controller updates ($\operatorname{permits}(v_1)\subseteq\operatorname{permits}(v_0)$ absent a fresh grant), and isolated vulnerability search \cite{scientist}. In our executable $\VThreeJarvisStages$-state \texttt{DiscoveryLedger} over $\VThreeJarvisPeriods$ periods, vulnerability discovery, validation, and mitigation confer zero authority or risk-bound credit until validated cut deployment: sealed cut closure leaves $\VThreeJarvisSealedBacklog$ unclosed findings and $\VThreeJarvisSealedExposure$ residual exposure, versus $\VThreeJarvisLaggedBacklog$ backlog and $\VThreeJarvisLaggedExposure$ exposure under lagged patching. Across a $\VThreePersonasCells$-cell disposition $\times$ boundary synthesis ($\VThreePersonasEpisodes$ scripted episodes/cell; Tier~4 proposal mechanics), fenced verified gates hold modeled Tier-1 breaches to $\VThreePersonasFencedTierOneViolations$ across all four dispositions with $\VThreePersonasCoopFencedTaskPct\%$ cooperative task completion (vs.\ $\VThreePersonasCoopStackedTaskPct\%$ under stacked 6-family review's false-block tax), and pairing fenced mediation with \texttt{DiscoveryLedger} cut closure cuts adversarial unmodeled Tier-2 harm from $\VThreePersonasAdvOmittedTierTwoPct\%$ to $\VThreePersonasAdvClosedTierTwoPct\%$.

\section{Twelve-Field Authority Record, Upgrade Renewal Matrix, and Verification Ledger}

\subsection{Operational twelve-field authority and legitimacy record}
Any deployment claiming bounded reach under Conditions C1--C6 should maintain a versioned, externally auditable record across twelve mandatory fields. Recording \emph{Unknown} is valid and required whenever evidence is absent; an \emph{Unknown} entry blocks the authority scope that depends on that premise rather than defaulting to permission.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Purpose, Owners, and Affected Parties:} Intended task, direct beneficiaries, third parties and communities bearing externalized risk or resource burdens, accountable human sponsor, independent reviewer, and unilateral halt holders.
\item \textbf{Jurisdiction and Legitimacy:} Legal and moral standing of every grant issuer; required individual and collective quorums; unresolved jurisdictional disputes; accessible contestation, appeal, and remedy procedures.
\item \textbf{Mediated Scope and Channels:} Permitted action types, destinations, data classes, credentials, shared stores, RPC framing caps, and every prose, voice, or user-interface channel reaching human reviewers.
\item \textbf{Cryptographic Identity:} Model checkpoint and tokenizer digests, runtime and verifier binary SHA-256 digests, tool schemas, active policy revision, and context/memory retention rules.
\item \textbf{Conferred Authority and Revocation Semantics:} Per-action caps, cross-copy cumulative budget $B$, reservation uncertainty envelope $\mathcal{B}_t$, expiration timestamps, sub-delegation attenuation rules ($\operatorname{permits}_{\mathrm{child}} \subseteq \operatorname{permits}_{\mathrm{parent}}$), relational capability mutexes, and the distinction between broker admission withdrawal and durable sink epoch fencing (including expected lock-wait latency and non-refunded timeout quarantine).
\item \textbf{Verified Properties and Trusted Base:} Formally proved invariants, exact boundary between executable code (\texttt{exec}) and ghost trace specifications (\texttt{spec}), trusted adapters (wire adapters, SQLite write-ahead log, operating-system kernel, cooperative sink idempotence), and known specification omissions.
\item \textbf{Evaluated Usefulness and Operational Tax:} Legitimate positive tasks, non-abstention semantic oracles, compound benign false-block rates across gate depth $k$, synchronous fencing delay, and stranded-budget fraction.
\item \textbf{Adversarial and Audit Measurements:} Empirical joint miss rate $J_k$ under coordinated surrogate, score-feedback, and candidate-retry attacks; planted-pathway Fisher exact audit power across $(P, q_0, r)$; campaign-level clustering; frozen holdout digest.
\item \textbf{Transfer and Excluded Regimes:} Capability class covered by current measurements, excluded stronger or colluding adversaries, and unmodeled physical or social bypasses.
\item \textbf{Operations, Custody, and External Audit:} Append-only sink effect receipts, observed monitoring eligibility, sealed discovery workflow enforcing Custody Rules R1--R6 (cut-only disclosure prior to deployment), halt/recovery drill logs, and outside audit access.
\item \textbf{Ecological Footprint and Territorial Consent:} Scoped electricity, cooling water, critical-mineral, and land accounts by site and period; affected non-user communities; prior informed consent procedures and withdrawal terms \cite{kogui,iea}.
\item \textbf{Knowledge Provenance and Irreversibility:} Source provenance, CARE collective data governance terms \cite{carroll}, permitted purposes, and an explicit pre-consent statement of what token revocation cannot undo once data are disclosed or distilled into weights.
\end{enumerate}

\para{Automated reproducibility and cleanliness verification.}
Every build executes an automated verification pipeline that checks formatting and static analysis, unit and integration test suites, Verus SMT proof obligations and Z3 soundness mutation rejection, finite-state and multi-process crash/partition linearizability, cryptographic SHA-256 provenance of reference outputs against source code, byte-exact synchronization between reference data and generated tables/figures, and deterministic three-pass PDF compilation enforcing sequential citation ordering, typographic fit, and absence of internal file paths or unrendered tokens.

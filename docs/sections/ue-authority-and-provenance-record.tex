\subsection{Operational twelve-field authority and legitimacy record}
Any deployment claiming bounded reach under Conditions C1--C6 should maintain a versioned, externally auditable record across twelve mandatory fields. Recording \texttt{Unknown} is valid and required whenever evidence is absent; an \texttt{Unknown} entry blocks the authority scope that depends on that premise rather than defaulting to permission.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Purpose, Owners, and Affected Parties:} Intended task, direct beneficiaries, third parties and communities bearing externalized risk or resource burdens, accountable human sponsor, independent reviewer, and unilateral halt holders.
\item \textbf{Jurisdiction and Legitimacy:} Legal and moral standing of every grant issuer; required individual and collective quorums; unresolved jurisdictional disputes; accessible contestation, appeal, and remedy procedures.
\item \textbf{Mediated Scope and Channels:} Permitted action types, destinations, data classes, credentials, shared stores, RPC framing caps, and every prose, voice, or UI channel reaching human reviewers.
\item \textbf{Cryptographic Identity:} Model checkpoint and tokenizer digests, runtime and verifier binary SHA-256 hashes, tool schemas, active policy revision, and context/memory retention rules.
\item \textbf{Conferred Authority and Revocation Semantics:} Per-action caps, cross-copy cumulative budget $B$, reservation uncertainty envelope $\mathcal{B}_t$, expiration timestamps, sub-delegation attenuation rules ($\operatorname{permits}_{\mathrm{child}} \subseteq \operatorname{permits}_{\mathrm{parent}}$), and the distinction between broker admission withdrawal and durable sink epoch fencing (including expected lock-wait latency and non-refunded timeout quarantine).
\item \textbf{Verified Properties and Trusted Base:} Formally proved invariants, exact boundary between executable code (\texttt{exec}) and ghost trace specifications (\texttt{spec}), trusted adapters (parsers, SQLite WAL, OS kernel, cooperative sink idempotence), and known specification omissions.
\item \textbf{Evaluated Usefulness and Operational Tax:} Legitimate positive tasks, non-abstention semantic oracles, compound benign false-block rates across gate depth $k$, synchronous fencing delay, and stranded-budget fraction.
\item \textbf{Adversarial and Audit Measurements:} Empirical joint miss rate $J_k$ under coordinated surrogate, score-feedback, and candidate-retry attacks; planted-pathway Fisher exact audit power across $(P, q_0, r)$; campaign-level clustering; frozen holdout digest.
\item \textbf{Transfer and Excluded Regimes:} Capability class covered by current measurements, excluded stronger or colluding adversaries, and unmodeled physical or social bypasses.
\item \textbf{Operations, Custody, and External Audit:} Append-only sink effect receipts, observed monitoring eligibility, \texttt{DiscoveryLedger} stage enforcing Custody Rules R1--R6 (cut-only disclosure prior to deployment), halt/recovery drill logs, and outside audit access.
\item \textbf{Ecological Footprint and Territorial Consent:} Scoped electricity, cooling water, critical-mineral, and land accounts by site and period; affected non-user communities; prior informed consent procedures and withdrawal terms \cite{kogui,iea}.
\item \textbf{Knowledge Provenance and Irreversibility:} Source provenance, CARE collective data governance terms \cite{carroll}, permitted purposes, and an explicit pre-consent statement of what token revocation cannot undo once data are disclosed or distilled into weights.
\end{enumerate}

\para{Local verification gate (\texttt{make required}).}
Executing \texttt{make required} (\path{scripts/required.sh}) runs all checks strictly inside \path{target/} (never \path{/tmp}): \texttt{cargo fmt -{}-check}, \texttt{cargo clippy -D warnings}, all Rust unit/integration/interleaving tests, all Python \texttt{unittest} suites, fresh quick runs and source-hash validations of V1 (\path{scripts/run.py} + \path{scripts/validate.py}), V2 (\path{scripts/run_v2.py} + \path{scripts/analyze_v2.py} \texttt{-{}-source} on Linux, including pinned Verus verification and $6$ Z3 soundness mutations via \path{scripts/verify_core.py}), and V3--V5 (\path{scripts/run_v3.py} \texttt{-{}-quick} + \path{scripts/validate_v3.py} \texttt{-{}-source}), reference validations against \texttt{12b5579} and current checkout sources, byte-exact LaTeX table/figure synchronization (\path{scripts/paper.py} \texttt{-{}-check}), and deterministic 3-pass PDF compilation (\path{scripts/build_paper.py}, \texttt{SOURCE\_DATE\_EPOCH=1794340800}) enforcing sequential \texttt{[1]..[N]} citation ordering and rejecting unresolved references, font substitutions, missing glyphs, or overfull boxes $>2.0$\,pt. Linux CI executes on the \texttt{neverhuman-lan} runner group (\texttt{[self-hosted, Linux, X64, lan-ci, ubuntu24]}) while retaining portable functional verification on \texttt{macos-latest}.

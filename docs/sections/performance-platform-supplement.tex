\subsection{Historical platforms, height cues, open microdata, and benchmark audits}\label{sec:platformmethods}

\emph{POV eligibility, commitment, and childbearing-intention audits.} We scanned all \PboPovRows{} source rows in the
historical POV dating extract and verified that each account key was present and unique. The main life-course analysis uses
recorded ages 18--79 (\PboPovEligible{} profiles seeking the other recorded gender: exact labels \texttt{Female} seeking
\texttt{Man}, and \texttt{Male} seeking \texttt{Woman}). ``Yes'' and ``All my kids are over 18'' count as parenthood,
``No'' defines the childless comparison group, and other entries have unknown parenthood (Fig.~\ref{fig:pbo-pov}). The three
substantive childbearing-intention answers are ``Want children,'' ``Does not want children,'' and ``Undecided/Open'';
``Prefer Not To Say'' and blanks count as nonresponse. Table~\ref{tab:pbo-pov-responses} reports all response shares and
denominators at ages 25--29 and 45--49, and Table~\ref{tab:pbo-pov-sensitivity} verifies the childbearing-intention
crossover across exact-age standardization, single-status filtering, all seeking categories, and answered-only cohorts. Five
national sub-cohorts (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Brazil, Australia) have sufficient sample depth in every age-by-sex
cell ($\ge\PboMinCell$) for independent country-level replication (Table~\ref{tab:pbo-pov-geo}).

\emph{POV stature parsing and rounding-cliff audit.} Self-reported stature in POV is recorded in imperial feet/inches with
metric equivalents (e.g.\ \texttt{6' 0" (183cm)}). Across all \PboPovEligible{} opposite-sex-seeking adult profiles, we
partitioned records into four mutually exclusive categories: missing/blank, sentinel entries (\texttt{< 5' 0"} or
\texttt{999cm}), implausible outliers outside $5'0''$--$7'0''$ ($60$--$84$~inches), and explicit plausible heights. Among
men, \PboHeightMenN{} profiles carry explicit heights between $5'0''$ and $7'0''$. Within-sex height percentiles assign each
inch bin its midpoint cumulative rank, and Gaussian expected bin shares integrate a normal distribution matched to the
empirical male mean and standard deviation. This audit isolates the sharp heaping at $5'11''$ (\PboHeightFiveElevenN) and
$6'0''$ (\PboHeightSixZeroN, $\PboHeightSixZeroRatio\times$ Gaussian expectation) followed by the $-\PboHeightCliffDrop$
drop at $6'1''$ (\PboHeightSixOneN; Table~\ref{tab:pbo-height}).

\emph{HON eligibility, image-inventory reconciliation, and cross-national coverage.} We scanned \PboHonRows{} canonical
profiles in the HON rating archive. Of these, \PboHonEligible{} satisfy recorded age 18--79, recorded \texttt{Female} or
\texttt{Male}, unconflicted metadata and vote labels, and at least 20 valid votes with $0\le\text{likes}\le\text{votes}$.
Sensitivities vary the vote floor to $\ge 1$ and $\ge 100$ votes, compare equal-profile means against vote-pooled rates, and
restrict to recorded United States accounts (Table~\ref{tab:pbo-hon-sensitivity}). Figure~\ref{fig:pbo-ratings} compares all
eligible profiles against the \PboHonImages{} eligible profiles linked to at least one file in the \PboHonFiles-image
inventory: saved-image coverage is higher for men (\PboImageShareM) than women (\PboImageShareW) and selects profiles with
lower female like rates, demonstrating why full-archive denominators are essential. Across recorded geography,
\PboHonCountriesHundred{} countries and territories contain at least 100 eligible profiles per sex,
\PboHonCountriesFiveHundred{} contain at least 500 per sex (used for the participation-ratio vs.\ like-gap test,
$r=\PboHonPartLikeCorr$), and \PboHonCountriesStrictAge{} satisfy $\ge 100$ profiles in every one of the four sex-by-age
cells (\texttt{Female}/\texttt{Male} $\times$ ages 18--29 / 40--59), in \PboHonFemaleSteeperCountries{} of which women's
within-gender beauty rank drops more steeply with age than men's (median drop \PboHonAgeDropMedW{} vs.\ \PboHonAgeDropMedM{}
points).

\emph{Open microdata, meta-analytic synthesis, and multimodal benchmark audits.} Four checksum-verified open deposits
complement the platform and Columbia speed-dating (\PboSpeedN{} peer-ranked decisions across \PboSpeedSessions{} sessions)
reanalyses. First, in Whyte et al.~\cite{whyte2021} ($N=\PboWhyteN$ Australians aged 18--64: \PboWhyteMenN{} men,
\PboWhyteWomenN{} women), stated partner importance on 0--100 scales was evaluated both raw and person-mean-centered across
nine traits, and self-rated sexual attractiveness was converted to midpoint percentiles within sex to test linear and
quadratic stature gradients as well as standardized multivariate regressions controlling for age, health, income, and
education (with heterosexual-only sensitivity confirming curvilinearity). Second, in Lidborg, Cross, and
Boothroyd~\cite{lidborg2022}, effect sizes were oriented so positive $r$ denotes higher mating or reproductive success,
converted via Fisher's $z$, and synthesized by DerSimonian--Laird random-effects models alongside medians and
sample-size-weighted means across mating (\PboLidborgHeightMatK{} height effects, $N=\PboLidborgHeightMatN$; 121 bodily
strength effects, $N=38{,}330$) and reproduction (\PboLidborgHeightRepK{} height effects, $N=\PboLidborgHeightRepN$; 14
bodily strength effects, $N=1{,}355$). Third, in Kowal et al.~\cite{kowal2025}, exact reproduction of the 117,293-row
deposit traced successive quality filters to \PboKowalBudgetN{} respondents whose six mate-budget allocations summed to 30,
yielding $62{,}185$ adults across \PboKowalCountryN{} countries with $\ge 100$ men and $\ge 100$ women (and
\PboKowalStdCountryN{} countries meeting $\ge 25$ per sex in every age band for exact-age-standardized sensitivity),
alongside \PboKowalBridgeN{} countries overlapping directly with HON ($\ge 100$/sex). Fourth, Table~\ref{tab:pbo-ai-bench}
audits the 20-wave HON facial-rating benchmark ($10{,}000$ profiles, $20{,}000$ images, strict profile-grouped train/test
split), tracking held-out Spearman $\rho$ from raw UV textures (H1: $\rho=\PboBenchBaselineR$) through nuisance removal,
domain face encoders, canonical $106$-landmark multi-backbone pure-face scoring with zero metadata
($\rho=\PboFacePureSpearman$, $\rho^2=\PboFacePureRankVar$), cross-modal tabular conditioning, and multi-photo aggregation to
the constrained grand ensemble (H20: $\rho=\PboBenchFinalR$, $\rho^2=0.438$), leaving \PboBenchUnexplainedPct{} of
human-jury rank variance unexplained.

\emph{Canonical $106$-landmark Delaunay super-resolution and $12$-zone Shapley facial attribution.} To audit what anatomical
and surface features drive single-context facial consensus without ever storing or displaying an identifiable person, we
evaluated \PboFaceTotalN{} primary $1200\times 1200$ aligned HON portraits stratified across sex, five adult age bands
(`18--24`, `25--34`, `35--44`, `45--54`, `55+`), within-gender rating deciles, and \PboFaceWorldRegionsN{} world regions,
using the unified within-gender empirical cumulative distribution of all \PboHonImages{} eligible image-inventoried profiles.
Each source portrait was mapped via `AuraFace` $106$-landmark detection~\cite{deng2019arcface} onto a symmetric canonical
$106$-vertex, \PboFaceTrisN-triangle Delaunay mesh ($224\times 224$ analytical space and $384\times 384$ studio portrait
frame) by exact piecewise-affine triangle warping, recording per-triangle log-Jacobian area strain $\log|\det J_t|$. Cohort
portraits were averaged in linear sRGB ($\gamma=2.2$) with every displayed composite aggregating $N\ge 75$ aligned individuals
(exceeding our $N\ge 50$ privacy floor). On the $10{,}000$-profile ($20{,}000$-image) benchmark with strict profile-grouped
train/test splits and Empirical-Bayes Beta-Binomial vote shrinkage, concatenating `DINOv3-ViT-B/16`~\cite{oquab2024dinov2},
`ConvNeXt V2`~\cite{woo2023convnextv2}, `AuraFace` `Glint360K-R100`~\cite{deng2019arcface}, and $106$-landmark morphometric
features with zero profile metadata yields held-out $\rho=\PboFacePureSpearman$ (percentile MAE \PboFacePurePctMae). Across
the \PboFaceTotalN{} live-warped portraits, we fitted a multi-backbone beauty head (`DINOv3-ViT-S/16` + `SFace` +
`genderage` + local CIE-Lab/sharpness/symmetry + \PboFaceTrisN-triangle strain) strictly on training profiles (held-out
$\rho=\PboFaceLiveSpearmanW$ in women, $\rho=\PboFaceLiveSpearmanM$ in men) and decomposed predictions across $12$ canonical
anatomical zones via a joint interventional Shapley coalition combiner~\cite{lundberg2017shap} centered on the neutral
42nd--58th percentile training cohort (combining zero-receptive-field local Lab/contrast/wrinkle/asymmetry/averageness
descriptors with localized `DINOv3` spatial tokens, projected onto $384\times 384$ portraits via a $12\times 12$ dual-basis
operator and verified against three-backbone counterfactual zone inpainting and $600$-draw bootstrap 95\% confidence
intervals; Figs.~\ref{fig:pbo-face-drivers}--\ref{fig:pbo-face-world} and Table~\ref{tab:pbo-face-drivers}).

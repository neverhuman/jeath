\section{Intelligence Proposes; Institutions Confer and Revoke Reach}
\label{sec:intro}

Intelligence is what a system can propose; authority is what external state transitions
it can execute, through which independent checkpoints, and what residual effects remain
committable after permission is withdrawn \cite{dennis,saltzer,schneider}. Much of the
superintelligence alignment literature treats safety as an internal property of a
model's learned disposition: if training could fix what an arbitrarily capable optimizer
wants, institutions could trust what it does. Yet instrumental incentives toward
resource acquisition, goal preservation, and shutdown avoidance arise across broad
objective classes \cite{omohundro2008,bostrom2014,soares2015}, and every behavioral
evaluation observes only a strategic interaction with the evaluated policy. As search
capability grows, a policy that shares its operator's aims and a policy that recognizes
an evaluation leave indistinguishable transcript records \cite{needham,schoen}. Human
institutions solved the analogous problem without mind-reading: banks do not certify the
inner virtue of every clerk, and operating systems do not trust user processes to police
their own memory \cite{saltzer,sel4}. They bound reach through exogenous capabilities
that are conferred, scoped, cryptographically bound, and revocable.

Making revocable authority operational against superintelligent optimization pressure
exposes two sharp gaps (Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}). First, \emph{endogenous safety
classifiers degrade with proposer search width}: if a transcript or learned reviewer has
even a small blindspot $J>0$ over safety-violating payloads, evaluating $K_{\mathrm{opt}}$
candidate phrasings or tool chains drives bypass probability to
$1-(1-J)^{K_{\mathrm{opt}}}\to 1$ (Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}b). Second, \emph{stopping
token generation does not revoke work in flight} \cite{controlroom}: revoking admission
at a permission broker leaves previously issued capabilities executing across concurrent
subagents, while refunding timed-out reservations under lost replies permits double
spending \cite{ftbudget,limbo}. Bounding authority across capability scaling therefore
requires coordinating two exogenous mechanisms across separate durable stores
(Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}): a broker that reserves cumulative budgets \emph{before}
dispatch inside a conservative state-uncertainty envelope $\B_t$, and cooperative
idempotent sinks whose signed epoch fences and bounded leases exclude post-revocation
commits regardless of proposer intelligence.

This monograph unifies mechanized protocol verification, superintelligence scaling laws,
multi-process cryptographic durability, exact small-sample inference, and co-evolutionary
incentive geometry into a five-act assurance framework across three orthogonal variables:
\emph{capability} (the richness and search width of proposals $a_t$), \emph{aim} (the
objective selected by post-training or tournament geometry), and \emph{authority} (the
external state transitions admitted by an independent sink).

\para{Five-act roadmap.}
\textbf{Act~I} (Sections~\ref{sec:intro}--\ref{sec:evidence}) establishes why evaluation
awareness, monitor subversion, and reactive output stops require exogenous mediation,
summarizing our seven Macro KPIs (Table~\ref{tab:v5kpis}), five evidence tiers, and six
sufficiency conditions (\textbf{C1--C6}, Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy}). \textbf{Act~II}
(Section~\ref{sec:theory}; proofs in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}) proves audit-volume
scaling, uncertainty contraction over $\B_t$, optimization-pressure invariance across
$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$, multi-agent smurfing bounds, typed covert-channel contraction,
exogenous lease-fenced off-switch corrigibility, and Theorem~\ref{u03:thm:main}.
\textbf{Act~III} (Sections~\ref{sec:core}--\ref{sec:eval}; Appendices~\ref{app:protocol}
and~\ref{app:predeclared}) presents the \VTwoProofs-obligation Verus kernel (total wire
bijection, $d$-hop attenuation, self-upgrade non-escalation, and cluster quorum fencing),
validates a three-process Ed25519/Macaroon SQLite WAL cluster across
\VFiveClusterFuzzFrames{} wire-fuzz frames and \VFiveClusterSigkillCycles{} hard
\texttt{SIGKILL} restarts, and pairs preserved predeclared $N{=}\VFourHoldoutCasesPerCheckpoint$
\texttt{Qwen3} campaigns with exact unconditional Suissa--Shuster/Tango and anytime-valid
Ville supermartingale certificates across \VFiveStatGrandPairs{} live runs
(Figure~\ref{fig:v5hero}c). \textbf{Act~IV}
(Sections~\ref{sec:graphs}--\ref{sec:selection}; Appendix~\ref{app:simulations}) bounds
AgentDojo/Linux pathway cutsets, Good--Turing withholding non-identifiability,
\VFourTrajEvalCount{} 5-step trajectory monitors, and evolutionary plus 412-parameter
neural RL selection geometry. \textbf{Act~V}
(Sections~\ref{sec:legitimacy}--\ref{sec:conclusion}; Appendix~\ref{app:record})
separates enforceability from specification adequacy and institutional standing, audits
the trusted computing base, and states ten falsifiable predictions.

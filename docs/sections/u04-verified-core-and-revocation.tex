\subsection{Pre-Dispatch Reservation and Uncertainty Quarantine}
Let $B$ denote cumulative budget, $C_{\mathrm{comm}}$ the charge of committed sink
effects, and $I_t$ the unresolved issued permits with positive maximum charges
$\{q_i\}_{i \in I_t}$. Because any subset of $I_t$ may already have committed or still
arrive, true spend lies in
$\mathcal{B}_t = [C_{\mathrm{comm}},\, C_{\mathrm{comm}} + \sum_{i \in I_t} q_i]$, and
broker charged authority $C = C_{\mathrm{comm}} + \sum_{i \in I_t} q_i$ tracks the exact
upper endpoint of $\mathcal{B}_t$.

Before any permit crosses the dispatch channel, the broker executes an immediate write
transaction checking overflow-safe capacity ($q > 0$, $q \le B$, $C \le B - q$), persists
the pending reservation record, and sets $C' = C + q$. Persisting that record linearizes
admission; retrying an identical request after a post-commit broker crash returns the
stored pending permit without charging twice. Crucially, reservation release accepts no
timeout parameter: ambiguous outcomes---in-flight status, unknown sink lookup, channel
closure, or a lost reply after a post-commit sink crash---never refund $C$. Only an
authoritative terminal sink \emph{Cancelled} receipt releases $q$, while \emph{Committed}
settles $q$ permanently into consumed spend, trading temporary budget availability under
faults for strict cumulative conservation.

\subsection{Admission vs.\ Quorum-Fenced Revocation and $d$-Hop Attenuation}
Revocation operates independently per required issuer $j \in \{0,\dots,n-1\}$ and
separates broker issuance from sink execution:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Admission revocation.} At broker instant $r$, withdrawing issuer $j$
atomically increments epoch counter $e_{\mathrm{iss}}[j]$, marks issuer $j$ inactive, and
clears issuer-$j$ grant bits across approvals and delegations (never restored by
subsequent renewal). New old-epoch issuance halts immediately, leaving post-withdrawal
effect exposure from in-flight permits $P_r$ bounded under sink deduplication
($0 \le e_i \le q_i$) by retained reservations:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in P_r} e_i \;\le\; \sum_{i \in P_r} q_i.
\label{eq:admission-exposure}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Quorum and lease-fenced sink revocation.} Across $M$ cooperative sink
shards, each shard $m$ enforces a signed lease expiry $L_m$ alongside a serialized
immediate-write fence transaction advancing epoch floor
$F_m[j] \leftarrow \max(F_m[j], \mathrm{floor})$, cancelling pending permits below the
floor, and recording durable \emph{Cancelled} tombstones on late arrivals. Once every
shard has either returned an Ed25519-signed fence acknowledgment ($F_m[j] \ge \mathrm{floor}$)
or passed its bounded lease horizon ($t \ge L_m$), quorum acknowledgment instant $a$
guarantees strictly zero post-acknowledgment commits under old authority across the
entire cluster:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{m=1}^{M}\sum_{i \in P_{r,m}} e_{m,i}^{(>a)} \;=\; 0.
\label{eq:fenced-zero}
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

For recursive subagent spawning and self-modification \cite{dennis}, an arbitrary-depth
chain $G_0 \to G_1 \to \dots \to G_d$ of scope grants enforces stepwise attenuation
($B_{k} \le B_{k-1}$, $T_k \le T_{k-1}$, $\mathrm{mask}_k \ \& \ \neg\mathrm{mask}_{k-1} = 0$,
and $\mathrm{req}_{k-1}[j] \Rightarrow \mathrm{req}_k[j]$). Strengthening a required
issuer at any hop binds immutably into canonical wire permits and chained Macaroon
caveats, so withdrawing \emph{any} ancestor issuer $j^*$ severs the entire descendant
subtree at the sink fence, while controller self-upgrades $v_0 \to v_1$ require either
scope inclusion ($G(v_1) \preceq G(v_0)$) or a fresh human quorum grant.

\subsection{Kernel Proposer Confinement into a Narrow Mediation Waist}
To prevent an untrusted Linux proposer from bypassing the broker over raw sockets or shared
memory, each proposer runs inside an unprivileged kernel sandbox without user namespaces
\cite{landlock,seccomp}. Its standard-I/O pipe binds a fixed server-side session actor,
exposes only proposal submission and status queries, discards unmediated error streams,
clears inherited environment variables, closes descriptors above standard error, and caps
frames at \VTwoLauncherCapBytes{} bytes (\VTwoServiceCapBytes{} bytes on service channels
with a POSIX poll mid-frame deadline). Before executing the proposer binary, the launcher
sets the kernel no-new-privileges bit, installs a Landlock ABI~$\ge 4$ filesystem and TCP
denial ruleset, and installs an architecture-checked seccomp-BPF filter blocking network
sockets, cross-process inspection and signaling, asynchronous ring I/O, eBPF, keyrings,
resource-limit escalation, and System~V/POSIX inter-process communication.

\section{Total Wire Bijections, Attenuated Lineages, and Quorum Leases Mechanize Authority}
\label{sec:core}

Section~\ref{sec:theory} showed that bounding operational reach requires an exogenous
stateful gate whose admission rule contracts monotonically with state uncertainty
$\mathcal{B}_t$ (Condition~C1), remains invariant under proposer search width
$K_{\mathrm{opt}}$, and halts in-flight capability exercise upon withdrawal rather than
merely stopping token generation \cite{saltzer,schneider,controlroom} (Condition~C6).
Because practical tool execution spans separate OS processes without cross-service atomic
transactions \cite{ftbudget,limbo}, our protocol splits authority across independent
SQLite (\texttt{WAL}) stores \cite{sqlite-transaction,sqlite-wal}: a \emph{Broker}
(\path{broker.sqlite}) governing approvals, delegations, epochs, and reservations, and
one or more \emph{Cooperative Idempotent Sinks} (\path{sink.sqlite}) governing monotone
epoch floors, bounded leases, deduplication, versioned resources, and committed effects.

\subsection{Pre-Dispatch Reservation and Uncertainty Quarantine}
Let $B$ denote cumulative budget, $C_{\mathrm{comm}}$ the charge of committed sink
effects, and $I_t$ the unresolved issued permits with positive maximum charges
$\{q_i\}_{i \in I_t}$. Because any subset of $I_t$ may already have committed or still
arrive, true spend lies in
$\mathcal{B}_t = [C_{\mathrm{comm}},\, C_{\mathrm{comm}} + \sum_{i \in I_t} q_i]$, and
broker charged authority $C = C_{\mathrm{comm}} + \sum_{i \in I_t} q_i$ tracks the exact
upper endpoint of $\mathcal{B}_t$.

Before any permit crosses the dispatch pipe, the broker executes an immediate write
transaction checking overflow-safe capacity ($q > 0$, $q \le B$, $C \le B - q$), persists
the pending record, and sets $C' = C + q$. Persisting that record linearizes admission;
retrying an identical request after a post-commit broker crash returns the stored pending
permit without charging twice. Crucially, \texttt{release} takes no timeout parameter:
ambiguous outcomes---\texttt{pending}, \texttt{unknown} at a sink lookup, EOF, or
\texttt{lost\_reply} after a post-commit sink crash---never refund $C$. Only an
authoritative terminal sink \texttt{Cancelled} receipt releases $q$, while
\texttt{Committed} settles $q$ permanently into consumed spend, trading temporary budget
availability under faults for strict cumulative conservation.

\subsection{Admission vs.\ Quorum-Fenced Revocation and $d$-Hop Attenuation}
Revocation operates independently per required issuer $j \in \{0,\dots,n-1\}$ and
separates broker issuance from sink execution:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Admission revocation.} At broker instant $r$, \texttt{withdraw}$(j)$
atomically increments $\texttt{epochs}[j]$, sets $\texttt{live}[j] \leftarrow \mathrm{false}$,
and clears issuer-$j$ grant bits across approvals and delegations (never restored by
subsequent \texttt{renew}$(j)$). New old-epoch issuance halts, leaving post-withdrawal
effect exposure from in-flight permits $P_r$ bounded under sink deduplication
($0 \le e_i \le q_i$) by retained reservations:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{i \in P_r} e_i \;\le\; \sum_{i \in P_r} q_i.
\label{eq:admission-exposure}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{Quorum and lease-fenced sink revocation.} Across $M$ cooperative sink
shards, each shard $m$ enforces a signed lease expiry $L_m$ alongside a serialized
\texttt{BEGIN IMMEDIATE} \texttt{fence}$(j, \mathrm{floor})$ transaction advancing
$\texttt{epoch\_floor}_m[j] \leftarrow \max(\texttt{epoch\_floor}_m[j], \mathrm{floor})$,
cancelling pending permits below the floor, and recording durable \texttt{Cancelled}
tombstones on late arrivals. Once every shard has either returned a signed fence
acknowledgment ($\texttt{epoch\_floor}_m[j] \ge \mathrm{floor}$) or passed its bounded
lease horizon ($t \ge L_m$), quorum acknowledgment instant $a$ guarantees strictly zero
post-acknowledgment commits under old authority across the entire cluster:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{m=1}^{M}\sum_{i \in P_{r,m}} e_{m,i}^{(>a)} \;=\; 0.
\label{eq:fenced-zero}
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

For recursive subagent spawning and self-modification \cite{dennis}, an arbitrary-depth
chain $G_0 \to G_1 \to \dots \to G_d$ of scope grants (\texttt{ExecScopeGrant}) enforces
stepwise attenuation ($B_{k} \le B_{k-1}$, $T_k \le T_{k-1}$,
$\mathrm{mask}_k \ \& \ \neg\mathrm{mask}_{k-1} = 0$, and
$\mathrm{req}_{k-1}[j] \Rightarrow \mathrm{req}_k[j]$). Strengthening a required issuer
at any hop binds immutably into canonical wire permits and chained Macaroon caveats, so
withdrawing \emph{any} ancestor issuer $j^*$ severs the entire descendant subtree at the
sink fence, while controller self-upgrades $v_0 \to v_1$ require either scope inclusion
($G(v_1) \preceq G(v_0)$) or a fresh human quorum grant.

\subsection{Kernel Proposer Confinement and Availability Scope}
\path{launch_v2.py} and \texttt{confine-v2} isolate untrusted Linux proposers without
user namespaces \cite{landlock,seccomp}. Each stdio pipe binds a fixed
\texttt{session\_actor}, exposes only \texttt{submit} and \texttt{status}, sets
\texttt{stderr=subprocess.DEVNULL}, clears the environment, and caps proposer frames at
\VTwoLauncherCapBytes{} bytes (\VTwoServiceCapBytes{} bytes on service pipes with a
POSIX \texttt{libc::poll} mid-frame slowloris deadline). Before \texttt{exec},
\texttt{confine-v2} sets \texttt{PR\_SET\_NO\_NEW\_PRIVS}, installs a 16-byte Landlock
ABI~$\ge 4$ filesystem/TCP ruleset, installs an architecture-checked seccomp-BPF
\texttt{EPERM} filter blocking sockets, \texttt{ptrace}, \texttt{process\_vm\_*},
\texttt{pidfd\_*}, signals, \texttt{io\_uring\_*}, \texttt{bpf}, keyrings, nonzero
\texttt{SYS\_prlimit64}, and System~V/POSIX IPC syscalls, and calls
\texttt{close\_range(3, ..)}.

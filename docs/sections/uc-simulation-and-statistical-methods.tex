\section{Synthetic Generative Specifications and Extended Tables}
\label{app:simulations}

This appendix documents the deterministic pure-Rust generative specifications, hyperparameters, and automated validation checks backing all sixteen Tier-4 reference artifacts in \texttt{evidence/v3/reference/*.json}, together with the extended V3 monitor and selection tables (Tables~\ref{tab:v3monitors} and~\ref{tab:v3selection}) and the preserved V1 two-family monitor baseline (Table~\ref{tab:monitors}).

\subsection{Deterministic Runtime, Numerical Primitives, and Scope}
All V3 simulations execute on CPU via \texttt{authority-lab run-v3 OUTPUT -{}-profile publication -{}-seed 20261110} (orchestrated by \texttt{scripts/run\_v3.py} and verified against source-parsed constants by \texttt{scripts/validate\_v3.py -{}-source}; fast CI gates use \texttt{-{}-profile smoke}). Pseudo-randomness derives exclusively from a standalone \texttt{SplitMix64} generator (\texttt{src/rng.rs}) seeded from $20261110$ with fixed domain-separation XOR/Weyl offsets per module, paired with a pure-Rust 128-layer Marsaglia--Tsang Ziggurat normal sampler, Cody rational \texttt{erfc}, Acklam--Halley normal quantile \texttt{norm\_ppf}, and a 200-node Golub--Welsch symmetric tridiagonal QL quadrature solver~\cite{golub}.

Two methodological boundaries apply across all Tier-4 artifacts. First, every classifier, graph solver, statistical test, and evolutionary loop is implemented from scratch in standalone Rust with zero third-party machine-learning or scientific crates; the monitor suite is therefore an independent synthetic boundary testbed rather than a \texttt{scikit-learn} bit-replica or a frontier-LLM correlation measurement. Second, elementary floating-point transcendentals (\texttt{exp}, \texttt{ln}, \texttt{sin}, \texttt{cos}, \texttt{tanh}) call the platform C math library (\texttt{libm}); publication evidence is pinned to Linux \texttt{x86\_64} reference outputs with SHA-256 digests recorded in \texttt{provenance.json}, without claiming unmeasured cross-architecture \texttt{libm} bit-identity.

\subsection{Artifact-by-Artifact Generative Specifications (16 JSON Files)}

\para{1--3. Run envelope and provenance.}
Artifacts \texttt{metadata.json}, \texttt{complete.json}, and \texttt{provenance.json} record run metadata and digests: \texttt{metadata.json} stores \texttt{schema\_version: 3}, \texttt{seed: 20261110}, \texttt{profile: "publication"} (\texttt{quick: false}), \texttt{rng: "splitmix64-v3"}, host OS/architecture, and \texttt{gpu\_used: false}. \texttt{complete.json} records clean termination and elapsed CPU wall time. \texttt{provenance.json} binds the git commit, exact invocation, UTC timestamp, SHA-256 hashes of \texttt{src/v3\_*.rs} and runner scripts, and SHA-256 hashes of all generated JSON files.

\para{4. Observation-lag contraction (\texttt{uncertainty.json}; \texttt{src/v3\_core\_models.rs}, H2).}
First, an exhaustive lattice check enumerates all $\text{\VThreeUncNestedPairs}$ nested integer interval pairs $[l_1,u_1] \subseteq [l_2,u_2] \subseteq [0, \VThreeUncBudget]$ under budget $B = \VThreeUncBudget$, verifying $U_{\I}([l_2,u_2]) \subseteq U_{\I}([l_1,u_1])$ with $\VThreeUncCounterexamples$ counterexamples. Second, seeded with $\texttt{seed} \oplus \texttt{0x554E434552543031}$, $\VThreeUncEpisodes$ paired 16-step episodes per cell ($120$ in \texttt{smoke}) draw proposals $a_t \in \{1,\dots,4\}$, process disturbances $w_t \in \{-1,0,1\}$, and self-report biases $\{-2,-1,0\}$ across observation lags $\Delta t \in \{0,1,2,4,8\}$ under four controllers: oracle state, sound conservative envelope $\B_t$ (accounting for worst-case unobserved spend across $\Delta t$ steps), stale point snapshot $\hat{x}_{t-\Delta t}$, and proposer self-report. Validation checks $\VThreeUncConsViolations$ conservative breaches across all lags and monotone admission contraction from $\VThreeUncConsAdmLagZeroPct\%$ ($\Delta t = 0$) to $\VThreeUncConsAdmLagEightPct\%$ ($\Delta t = 8$).

\para{5. Snapshot non-composition (\texttt{composition.json}; \texttt{src/v3\_core\_models.rs}, H6).}
Enumerates $(c, q_1, q_2) \in \{0,\dots,B\}^3$ across $B \in \{10, 20, 50, 100\}$ and cross-checks exact closed forms (Proposition~\ref{prop:snapshot-noncomp}): at $B = 100$ ($\text{\VThreeCompHundredTriples}$ triples), $\text{\VThreeCompHundredAdmissible}$ pairs are individually snapshot-admissible and $\text{\VThreeCompHundredOverspend}$ ($\VThreeCompHundredOverspendPct\%$) jointly overspend, versus $\VThreeCompHundredSerializedOverspend$ under serialized pre-dispatch reservation.

\para{6. Saturation, horizon summability, and tail halt (\texttt{risk\_horizon.json}; \texttt{src/v3\_core\_models.rs}, H9).}
Computes: (i)~Beta-mixture common-cause curves $J_k = c + (1-c)p^k$ ($u = \VThreeSatDefaultUPct\%$, $c = \VThreeSatDefaultCPct\%$, $p = 0.10$, $m = \VThreeSatDefaultMPct\%$, $k^* = \VThreeSatDefaultKStar$, $\VThreeAppAUnderstatement\times$ understatement at $k = 4$), continuous $\Theta \sim \mathrm{Uniform}[0,1]$ moments $1/(k+1)$, and capability-overtake curves $\rho(z) = \lambda_0 q_0 e^{(\beta-\alpha)z}$; (ii)~exact and Monte Carlo ($10{,}000$ trajectories; $2{,}000$ \texttt{smoke}) renewal breach curves up to $T = 10{,}000$ comparing summable $p_t = 0.01/t^2$ ($\VThreeHorizonSummableTenKPct\% \le \VThreeHorizonSummableBoundPct\%$), harmonic $0.01/t$ ($\VThreeHorizonHarmonicTenKPct\%$), and constant $10^{-3}$ ($\VThreeHorizonConstTenKPct\%$); and (iii)~heavy-tailed lognormal reactive stop latency ($20{,}000$ trials/load; $4{,}000$ \texttt{smoke}) against deadline $T_{\mathrm{irrev}} = \VThreeTailHaltWindowMs$\,ms across utilizations $\rho \in \{0.20, 0.50, 0.80, 0.95\}$ versus pre-commit leases with synchronous sink epoch fencing ($\VThreeTailHaltFencedViolations$ post-revoke violations).

\para{7--8. Discovery closure, endogenous grants, and personas (\texttt{jarvis\_ledger.json}, \texttt{personas.json}; \texttt{src/v3\_core\_models.rs}, H4, H5, H17).}
\texttt{jarvis\_ledger.json} exercises the $\VThreeJarvisStages$-state \texttt{DiscoveryLedger} (\texttt{Discovered} $\to$ \texttt{Triaged} $\to$ \texttt{Validated} $\to$ \texttt{MitigationReady} $\to$ \texttt{CutDeployed} $\to$ \texttt{Closed}) over $\VThreeJarvisPeriods$ periods ($64$ replicates; $16$ \texttt{smoke}), verifying zero authority expansion upon vulnerability discovery and contrasting immediate cut sealing ($\VThreeJarvisSealedBacklog$ unclosed backlog, $\VThreeJarvisSealedExposure$ exposure) with lagged patching ($\VThreeJarvisLaggedBacklog$ backlog, $\VThreeJarvisLaggedExposure$ exposure), alongside the endogenous vs.\ decoupled human grant matrix. \texttt{personas.json} evaluates $\VThreePersonasCells$ cells ($4$ scripted dispositions $\times$ $7$ boundary arms, $\VThreePersonasEpisodes$ episodes/cell; $100$ \texttt{smoke}), confirming $\VThreePersonasFencedTierOneViolations$ modeled Tier-1 breaches under fenced verified mediation and Tier-2 exposure reduction from $\VThreePersonasAdvOmittedTierTwoPct\%$ to $\VThreePersonasAdvClosedTierTwoPct\%$ after cut closure.

\para{9--10. Weighted cutsets and sparse DAG interdiction (\texttt{weighted\_cuts.json}, \texttt{interdiction.json}; \texttt{src/v3\_graphs.rs}, H10, H11).}
\texttt{weighted\_cuts.json} cross-checks our split-vertex Dinic solver against exhaustive $2^{|V_{\mathrm{elig}}|}$ subset search across $\text{\VThreeCutsBruteForceChecked}$ random weighted DAGs with ineligible vertices ($\VThreeCutsBruteForceDiscrepancies$ discrepancies), verifies $+\infty$ cost when an ungateable route bypasses $V_{\mathrm{elig}}$, and evaluates layered scaling families $G_A$ and $G_B$ for $k \in \{0,2,4,6\}$ ($\VThreeCutsMinVertices$--$\VThreeCutsMaxVertices$ internal vertices; $\text{\VThreeCutsMinPaths}$--$\text{\VThreeCutsMaxPaths}$ paths). \texttt{interdiction.json} generates $\VThreeInterGraphsPerFamily$ sparse 8-stage DAGs per family ($40$ \texttt{smoke}; seed $\texttt{seed} \oplus \texttt{0x494E544552443033}$) across \texttt{narrow} (waist width $6$, other layers $22$--$28$; median global min-cut $\VThreeInterNarrowWidth$), \texttt{hub} (median $\VThreeInterHubWidth$), and \texttt{wide} (layers $24$--$30$; median $\VThreeInterWideWidth$), sampling $N \in \{30, 100, 300, 1{,}000, 3{,}000\}$ low-cost routes under Boltzmann inverse temperature $\beta = 0.5$ via exact log-domain backward dynamic programming and evaluating global min-cut, reported-subgraph min-cut, degree heuristics, and route-specific bottleneck patching across budgets $B \in \{1,2,4,6,8,12,16,32\}$.

\para{11--12. Zipf coverage and planted-audit power (H12, H13).}
Implemented in \texttt{src/v3\_coverage.rs}, \texttt{coverage\_v3.json} simulates $K = \text{\VThreeCovClasses}$ Zipf classes ($p_i \propto (i+10)^{-1.05}$) across $N = \text{\VThreeCovReports}$ reports ($6$ searchers $\times 2{,}000$ draws, $\VThreeCovTrials$ replicates; $40$ \texttt{smoke}) under \texttt{honest}, \texttt{shared\_blind\_15pct} (exact $15\%$ unseen mass), and \texttt{withheld\_critical\_5pct} (strategic suppression of $5\%$ critical mass), comparing Good--Turing $\hat{C}_{\mathrm{GT}} = 1 - f_1/N$~\cite{good}, two-list Chapman capture--recapture~\cite{chapman}, and McAllester--Schapire vs.\ Clopper--Pearson bounds~\cite{mcallester,clopper}. \texttt{audit\_power.json} computes exact hypergeometric one-sided Fisher exact $p$-values~\cite{fisher} ($\alpha = \VThreeAuditAlphaPct\%$) for $P \in \{10, 20, 40, 80, 120, 200\}$ split equally between ordinary plants ($q_0 \in \{0.50, 0.80\}$) and salient plants ($q_1 = r q_0$, $r \in [0,1]$), verifying size $\le \alpha$ at $r = 1$ and exact binomial Clopper--Pearson upper bounds.

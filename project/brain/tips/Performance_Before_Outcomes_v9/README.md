# Performance Before Outcomes (final, version 9.0)

*It takes a village to read a mind: how being judged at everything, by people who knew us for years, could build a mind for anything, and what happens when the jury goes home* (Jepson Taylor and Erin Beilharz).
Not peer reviewed. Literature synthesis, mathematics, code, data analysis and drafting were AI-assisted; see the disclosure in the paper.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Performance_Before_Outcomes_v9.pdf` | The paper: a cover page, a fifteen-page article, Supplementary Information, references, author biographies. |
| `Performance_Before_Outcomes_v9.tex` | Single-file LaTeX source. Needs only `figures/`; run `pdflatex` twice. |

| `results/` | Machine-readable outputs of every experiment and analysis, plus `ref_verification.json` (Crossref record for each reference). |
| `data/` | Three open fossil datasets; see `data/README.md`. The General Social Survey file, the Columbia speed-dating file, the D-PLACE ethnographic codes and the 175- and 45-country survey files are downloaded by `code/fetch_gss.py`, `fetch_speed.py`, `fetch_dplace.py` and `fetch_open.py` and are not redistributed. |
| `Scorecard_and_Review_v9.pdf` | The rubric, the scores of every draft, the review of each, and the list of errors corrected. |

## Rebuilding

    cd code && ./run_all.sh

Requires Python 3.11+ with NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and openpyxl; `git` and network access the first time (to fetch the
survey file and the speed-dating file); and a LaTeX installation with `extarticle`, `mathpazo`, `natbib`, `titlesec`, `tabularx`, `booktabs`,
`caption`, `stfloats` and `flushend`. The figure scripts use the Inter typeface if it is installed and fall back to DejaVu
Sans otherwise.

Every analysis-derived number in the text is a macro in `paper/generated/numbers.tex`, written by `code/make_tex.py`
from `results/*.json`. The bibliography is generated by `code/build_bib.py` from Crossref (network access needed only to
regenerate it; `paper/refs.bib` is included).

## What is new in this version

* Second author, and author biographies with photographs after the references. To add the first author's photograph, save
  it as `paper/figures/author_taylor.jpg` (or `figures/author_taylor.jpg` beside the single-file source) and rebuild.
* Every note from the authors' review of version 8 is answered in the main text and, point by point, in SI section S8.
* Who decided (main text 2.9, Fig. 7, Table S14): the coded ethnographic record on who chooses a spouse, consent,
  coercion and divorce (`code/exp_cultures.py`, E18), and an equation for what coercion does to the mechanism.
* Who becomes an ancestor (Proposition 18): effective number over census number equals (1 - q)/(1 + alpha); the genetic
  "twice as many mothers" ratio is not a count of childless men (`code/exp_village.py`, `code/exp_notes.py`).
* The world (Fig. 8, Table S17): stated ideals in 45 countries, sex differences and a replication of "the door" in 70
  countries of a 175-country survey (`code/exp_world.py`, E20).
* The jury is being dismissed (main text 2.11, Fig. 9, Tables S15-S16): ideals, a decomposition of falling births, social
  ties, and Proposition 19 on waiting for evidence (`code/exp_village.py`, E19).
* Safe contests (Corollary 9), narrow tests (Corollary 10), shrinking brains and efficiency, firelight and storytelling.
* Eight stated conclusions (main text 3.1); a six-panel first page; 35 predictions; 51 objections; 19 propositions.
* New audit checks A14-A17. Everything from version 8 is retained.

## Scripts

`code/run_all.sh` runs everything. The analysis scripts are `exp_core.py`, `exp_rates.py`, `exp_power.py`,
`exp_contest.py`, `exp_polymath.py`, `exp_verify.py` (theory and simulation); `exp_gss.py`, `exp_village.py` (national
survey); `exp_speed.py` (speed dating); `exp_cultures.py` (ethnographic codes); `exp_world.py` (cross-national surveys);
`exp_notes.py` (checks of the version 9 results); `audit.py` (independent checks); `make_figures.py`; `make_tex.py`;
`build_bib.py` with `refs_list.py` (references); and the `fetch_*.py` scripts, which download and verify the public data.
